# Homework: Lessons 7–8 — Expressions and Equations
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This homework covers
[07-algebraic-expressions-and-like-terms.md](./07-algebraic-expressions-and-like-terms.md)

## Part A: Evaluating Expressions and Combining Like Terms

### Problem 1

Evaluate $5x - 3$ at $x = 6$.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$5(6) - 3 = 30 - 3 = 27$$

The answer is **$27$**.

</details>

### Problem 2

Evaluate $-2x + 9$ at $x = -5$.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$-2(-5) + 9 = 10 + 9 = 19$$

The answer is **$19$**.

</details>

### Problem 3

Simplify: $8x + 3x$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$8x + 3x = (8+3)x = 11x$$

The answer is **$11x$**.

</details>

### Problem 4

Simplify: $7x - 2 - 3x + 6$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Group the $x$-terms and constants separately:

$$\underbrace{7x - 3x}_{x\text{-terms}} + \underbrace{-2 + 6}_{\text{constants}} = 4x + 4$$

The answer is **$4x + 4$**.

</details>

### Problem 5

Simplify: $-6x + 4 - 2x - 9$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Group the $x$-terms and constants separately:

$$\underbrace{-6x - 2x}_{x\text{-terms}} + \underbrace{4 - 9}_{\text{constants}} = -8x - 5$$

The answer is **$-8x - 5$**.

</details>

## Part B: The Distributive Property

### Problem 6

Simplify: $5(x + 2)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$5(x + 2) = 5(x) + 5(2) = 5x + 10$$

The answer is **$5x + 10$**.

</details>

### Problem 7

Simplify: $-4(x - 3)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$-4(x - 3) = -4(x) + (-4)(-3) = -4x + 12$$

The answer is **$-4x + 12$**.

</details>

### Problem 8

Simplify: $3(x + 4) + 2x$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Distribute first:

$$3(x + 4) = 3x + 12$$

Combine with the remaining term:

$$3x + 12 + 2x = 5x + 12$$

The answer is **$5x + 12$**.

</details>

### Problem 9

Simplify: $-2(x - 5) - 4x + 1$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Distribute first:

$$-2(x - 5) = -2x + 10$$

Combine with the remaining terms:

$$-2x + 10 - 4x + 1 = \underbrace{(-2x-4x)}_{x\text{-terms}} + \underbrace{(10+1)}_{\text{constants}} = -6x + 11$$

The answer is **$-6x + 11$**.

</details>

### Problem 10

A gym charges a $\$10$ sign-up fee plus $\$4$ per visit. Write a simplified expression for the
total cost after $x$ visits, then evaluate it for $x = 5$.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Each visit costs $4$ dollars, plus the one-time $10$ dollar fee:

$$4x + 10$$

Evaluate at $x = 5$:

$$4(5) + 10 = 20 + 10 = 30$$

The answer is **$4x + 10$ dollars; $\$30$ when $x = 5$**.

</details>

## Part C: One-Step and Two-Step Equations

### Problem 11

Solve: $x + 12 = 5$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Subtract $12$ from both sides:

$$x + 12 - 12 = 5 - 12$$

$$x = -7$$

The answer is **$x = -7$**.

</details>

### Problem 12

Solve: $x - 9 = -14$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Add $9$ to both sides:

$$x - 9 + 9 = -14 + 9$$

$$x = -5$$

The answer is **$x = -5$**.

</details>

### Problem 13

Solve: $-6x = 42$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide both sides by $-6$:

$$\frac{-6x}{-6} = \frac{42}{-6}$$

$$x = -7$$

The answer is **$x = -7$**.

</details>

### Problem 14

Solve: $\dfrac{x}{5} = -8$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply both sides by $5$:

$$\frac{x}{5} \times 5 = -8 \times 5$$

$$x = -40$$

The answer is **$x = -40$**.

</details>

### Problem 15

Solve: $4x + 7 = -9$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Undo the addition first:

$$4x + 7 - 7 = -9 - 7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4x = -16$$

Undo the multiplication:

$$x = \frac{-16}{4} = -4$$

The answer is **$x = -4$**.

</details>

### Problem 16

Solve: $-5x - 8 = 12$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Undo the subtraction first:

$$-5x - 8 + 8 = 12 + 8 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad -5x = 20$$

Undo the multiplication:

$$x = \frac{20}{-5} = -4$$

The answer is **$x = -4$**.

</details>

### Problem 17

Solve: $5x - 2x + 3 = 15$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Combine the like terms on the left first:

$$5x - 2x + 3 = 15 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3x + 3 = 15$$

Undo the addition:

$$3x + 3 - 3 = 15 - 3 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3x = 12$$

Undo the multiplication:

$$x = \frac{12}{3} = 4$$

The answer is **$x = 4$**.

</details>

## Part D: Word Problems

### Problem 18

A number increased by $8$ equals $-3$. What is the number?

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $x$ be the number. "Increased by $8$" means add $8$:

$$x + 8 = -3$$

Subtract $8$ from both sides:

$$x = -3 - 8 = -11$$

The answer is **$-11$**.

</details>

### Problem 19

A phone plan charges a flat $\$20$ monthly fee plus $\$0.10$ per text message. A month's bill
came to $\$35$. How many text messages were sent that month?

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $x$ be the number of text messages. The total cost is the flat fee plus $\$0.10$ per text:

$$0.10x + 20 = 35$$

Undo the addition first:

$$0.10x + 20 - 20 = 35 - 20 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 0.10x = 15$$

Undo the multiplication:

$$x = \frac{15}{0.10} = 150$$

The answer is **$150$ text messages**.

</details>

### Problem 20

A rectangle's length is $5$ more than its width. Its perimeter is $34$. Let $x$ be the width,
write an equation for the perimeter, and solve for $x$.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The length is $x + 5$. A rectangle's perimeter is twice the width plus twice the length:

$$2x + 2(x+5) = 34$$

Distribute and combine like terms:

$$2x + 2x + 10 = 34 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4x + 10 = 34$$

Undo the addition:

$$4x + 10 - 10 = 34 - 10 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4x = 24$$

Undo the multiplication:

$$x = \frac{24}{4} = 6$$

The answer is **$x = 6$** (width $6$, length $11$).

</details>
