# Homework: Lesson 7 — Simplifying Expressions
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This independent homework practices the skills from
[Lesson 7](./07-algebraic-expressions-and-like-terms.md): use the distributive property,
calculate carefully with fractions, combine like terms, and write each result in simplest
form. These are expressions, not equations, so do **not** solve for $x$.

For every problem: distribute first, then combine like terms. Reduce numerical fractions in
your final answer.

## Part B: Distribute and Calculate With Fractions

### Problem 11

Simplify: $\dfrac{1}{2}(x+8)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac12(x+8)=\frac12x+\frac12(8)=\frac{x}{2}+4$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{x}{2}+4$**.
</details>

### Problem 12

Simplify: $\dfrac{3}{4}(x+12)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac34(x+12)=\frac{3x}{4}+\frac{36}{4}=\frac{3x}{4}+9$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{3x}{4}+9$**.
</details>

### Problem 13

Simplify: $\dfrac{2}{3}(x-9)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac23(x-9)=\frac{2x}{3}-\frac{18}{3}=\frac{2x}{3}-6$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{2x}{3}-6$**.
</details>

### Problem 14

</details>

## Part C: Distribute, Then Combine Like Terms

### Problem 21

Simplify: $3(x+2)+4x$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$3(x+2)+4x=3x+6+4x=7x+6$$

The answer is **$7x+6$**.
</details>

### Problem 22

Simplify: $2(5x+1)-2\left(\dfrac{2}{3}x+\dfrac{5}{6}\right)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$2(5x+1)-2\left(\frac23x+\frac56\right)=10x+2-\frac{4x}{3}-\frac53$$

$$=\frac{30x}{3}-\frac{4x}{3}+\frac{6}{3}-\frac{5}{3}=\frac{26x}{3}+\frac13$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{26x}{3}+\dfrac13$**.
</details>

### Problem 23

Simplify: $\dfrac{3x+1}{4}+2x-5$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{3x+1}{4}+2x-5=\frac{3x}{4}+\frac14+2x-5$$

$$=\frac{3x}{4}+\frac{8x}{4}+\frac14-\frac{20}{4}=\frac{11x}{4}-\frac{19}{4}$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{11x}{4}-\dfrac{19}{4}$**.
</details>

### Problem 24

Simplify: $\dfrac{3}{2}\left(4x-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)-\dfrac{1}{4}(8x+4)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac32\left(4x-\frac23\right)-\frac14(8x+4)=6x-1-2x-1=4x-2$$

The answer is **$4x-2$**.
</details>

### Problem 25

Simplify: $-3\left(2x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\dfrac{1}{2}(4x+6)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$-3\left(2x-\frac12\right)+\frac12(4x+6)=-6x+\frac32+2x+3$$

$$=-4x+\frac92$$

The answer is **$-4x+\dfrac92$**.
</details>

### Problem 26

Simplify: $\dfrac{5x-3}{6}+\dfrac{x+9}{3}+x-2$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{5x-3}{6}+\frac{x+9}{3}+x-2=\frac{5x-3}{6}+\frac{2x+18}{6}+\frac{6x-12}{6}$$

$$=\frac{13x+3}{6}$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{13x+3}{6}$**.
</details>

### Problem 27

Simplify: $\dfrac{2}{3}(3x-9)-\dfrac{x-1}{2}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac23(3x-9)-\frac{x-1}{2}=2x-6-\frac{x}{2}+\frac12$$

$$=\frac{3x}{2}-\frac{11}{2}$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{3x}{2}-\dfrac{11}{2}$**.
</details>

### Problem 28

Simplify: $\dfrac{3x+1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}(x-6)+3x$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{3x+1}{4}-\frac12(x-6)+3x=\frac{3x}{4}+\frac14-\frac{x}{2}+3+3x$$

$$=\frac{13x}{4}+\frac{13}{4}$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{13x}{4}+\dfrac{13}{4}$**.
</details>

### Problem 29

Simplify: $-\dfrac{1}{3}(6x-9)+\dfrac{5x-2}{4}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$-\frac13(6x-9)+\frac{5x-2}{4}=-2x+3+\frac{5x}{4}-\frac12$$

$$=-\frac{3x}{4}+\frac52$$

The answer is **$-\dfrac{3x}{4}+\dfrac52$**.
</details>

### Problem 30

Simplify: $\dfrac{3}{5}(5x+10)-2\left(\dfrac{x}{4}+\dfrac{3}{2}\right)+\dfrac{x-1}{3}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac35(5x+10)-2\left(\frac{x}{4}+\frac32\right)+\frac{x-1}{3}$$

$$=3x+6-\frac{x}{2}-3+\frac{x}{3}-\frac13$$

$$=\frac{17x}{6}+\frac83$$

The answer is **$\dfrac{17x}{6}+\dfrac83$**.
</details>
