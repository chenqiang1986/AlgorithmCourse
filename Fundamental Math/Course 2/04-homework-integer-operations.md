*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This homework covers
[03-negative-integers-addition-subtraction.md](./03-negative-integers-addition-subtraction.md)

## Part A: Adding and Subtracting Integers

### Problem 1

Evaluate: $(-23) + (-15)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Same sign (both negative), so add the absolute values and keep the negative sign:

$$(-23) + (-15) = -(23 + 15) = -38$$

The answer is **$-38$**.

</details>

### Problem 2

Evaluate: $34 + (-50)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Different signs, so subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger ($50 - 34 = 16$)
and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value ($-50$ is larger in
absolute value, and it's negative):

$$34 + (-50) = -(50 - 34) = -16$$

The answer is **$-16$**.

</details>

### Problem 3

Evaluate: $12 - (-19)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rewrite the subtraction as adding the opposite of $-19$, which is $19$:

$$12 - (-19) = 12 + 19 = 31$$

The answer is **$31$**.

</details>

### Problem 4

Evaluate: $-15 - (-8) - 6 + 20$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rewrite every subtraction as adding the opposite, then work left to right:

$$-15 - (-8) - 6 + 20 = -15 + 8 + (-6) + 20$$

$$\begin{aligned}
-15 + 8 &= -7 \\
-7 + (-6) &= -13 \\
-13 + 20 &= 7
\end{aligned}$$

The answer is **$7$**.

</details>

### Problem 5

The temperature was $-6°$F at midnight. By dawn it had dropped another $9$ degrees. What
was the temperature at dawn?

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

"Dropped $9$ degrees" means adding $-9$:

$$-6 + (-9) = -(6 + 9) = -15$$

The answer is **$-15°$F**.

</details>

### Problem 6

Point $A$ is at $-12$ on a number line and point $B$ is at $5$. Find $A - B$ and $B - A$.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$A - B$ is the signed distance from $B$ to $A$: walking from $5$ to $-12$ is $17$ units to
the left, so

$$A - B = -12 - 5 = -17$$

Reversing the two points reverses the direction of travel, so $B - A$ is the opposite:

$$B - A = 5 - (-12) = 17$$

The answers are **$A - B = -17$** and **$B - A = 17$**.

</details>

## Part B: Multiplying and Dividing Integers

### Problem 7

Evaluate: $(-9) \times 7$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Different signs, so the product is negative:

$$(-9) \times 7 = -(9 \times 7) = -63$$

The answer is **$-63$**.

</details>

### Problem 8

Evaluate: $(-11) \times (-6)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Same sign (both negative), so the product is positive:

$$(-11) \times (-6) = +(11 \times 6) = 66$$

The answer is **$66$**.

</details>

### Problem 9

Evaluate: $(-3) \times (-2) \times (-1) \times 4$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Count the negative factors: $-3$, $-2$, $-1$ — three negative factors, which is odd, so
the product is negative. Multiply the absolute values:

$$3 \times 2 \times 1 \times 4 = 24$$

Applying the negative sign:

$$(-3) \times (-2) \times (-1) \times 4 = -24$$

The answer is **$-24$**.

</details>

### Problem 10

Evaluate: $(-96) \div 8$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Different signs, so the quotient is negative:

$$(-96) \div 8 = -(96 \div 8) = -12$$

The answer is **$-12$**.

</details>

### Problem 11

Evaluate: $(-144) \div (-12)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Same sign (both negative), so the quotient is positive:

$$(-144) \div (-12) = +(144 \div 12) = 12$$

The answer is **$12$**.

</details>

### Problem 12

A submarine starts at sea level ($0$ m) and descends $8$ meters every minute for $5$
minutes. Write a multiplication expression for its altitude above the sea level after $5$ minutes, and evaluate
it.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Descending $8$ meters per minute is a change of $-8$ meters each minute; over $5$ minutes
that's

$$(-8) \times 5 = -(8 \times 5) = -40$$

The answer is **$-40$ m** (that is, $40$ meters below sea level).

</details>

## Part C: Combined Expressions

These expressions mix addition/subtraction with multiplication/division. Do the
multiplication and division first, left to right, then the addition and subtraction —
the same order-of-operations rule used for positive numbers, applied with the integer
sign rules from Parts A and B.

### Problem 13

Evaluate: $(-4) \times 3 + 7$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply first (different signs, negative product), then add:

$$(-4) \times 3 = -12$$

$$-12 + 7 = -5$$

The answer is **$-5$**.

</details>

### Problem 14

Evaluate: $20 - (-3) \times 5$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply first (different signs, negative product):

$$(-3) \times 5 = -15$$

Then subtract, which means adding the opposite of $-15$:

$$20 - (-15) = 20 + 15 = 35$$

The answer is **$35$**.

</details>

### Problem 15

Evaluate: $(-18) \div 3 - (-6)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide first (different signs, negative quotient):

$$(-18) \div 3 = -6$$

Then subtract, which means adding the opposite of $-6$:

$$-6 - (-6) = -6 + 6 = 0$$

The answer is **$0$**.

</details>

### Problem 16

Evaluate: $5 + (-2) \times (-4) \times 3$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply left to right first. $(-2) \times (-4)$ is same sign, positive:

$$(-2) \times (-4) = 8$$

$$8 \times 3 = 24$$

Then add:

$$5 + 24 = 29$$

The answer is **$29$**.

</details>

### Problem 17

Evaluate: $(-30) \div (-5) + (-9) \times 2$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide and multiply first, left to right:

$$(-30) \div (-5) = 6 \qquad \qquad (-9) \times 2 = -18$$

Then add:

$$6 + (-18) = -12$$

The answer is **$-12$**.

</details>

### Problem 18

Evaluate: $-7 \times (-2) - 15 \div (-3)$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply and divide first, left to right:

$$-7 \times (-2) = 14 \qquad \qquad 15 \div (-3) = -5$$

Then subtract, which means adding the opposite of $-5$:

$$14 - (-5) = 14 + 5 = 19$$

The answer is **$19$**.

</details>

### Problem 19

A vendor had already earned $\$50$ from sales. Then $6$ defective items were returned, each
costing the vendor $\$4$. Finally the vendor issued a $\$15$ refund for an unrelated
complaint. Write an expression for the vendor's total profit and evaluate it.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Six defective items at a $\$4$ loss each is $(-4) \times 6$; add the starting earnings, then
subtract the refund:

$$50 + (-4) \times 6 - 15$$

Multiply first:

$$(-4) \times 6 = -24$$

Then work left to right:

$$50 + (-24) = 26 \qquad \qquad 26 - 15 = 11$$

The answer is **$\$11$**.

</details>

### Problem 20

The temperature starts at $-3°$F, rises $2°$ per hour for $4$ hours, then suddenly drops
$5°$. Write an expression for the final temperature and evaluate it.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rising $2°$ per hour for $4$ hours is $2 \times 4$; add that to the starting temperature,
then subtract the drop:

$$-3 + 2 \times 4 - 5$$

Multiply first:

$$2 \times 4 = 8$$

Then work left to right:

$$-3 + 8 = 5 \qquad \qquad 5 - 5 = 0$$

The answer is **$0°$F**.

</details>
