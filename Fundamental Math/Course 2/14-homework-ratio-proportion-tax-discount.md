# Homework: Lesson 14 — Ratios, Proportions, Tax, and Discount Rates
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This homework accompanies [Lesson 14](./14-ratio-proportion-tax-discount.md). For every
problem, identify the part, the original price (the whole), and the rate. Write a
proportion, use cross products to make an equation, solve, and give money answers to the
nearest cent.

Use one of these forms:

$$\frac{\text{tax amount}}{\text{original price}}=\frac{\text{tax rate}}{100}$$

$$\frac{\text{discount amount}}{\text{original price}}=\frac{\text{discount rate}}{100}$$

## Part A: Tax Proportions

### Problem 1: Find a Tax Amount

**Problem.** A notebook has an original price of $\$25.00$. The tax rate is $4\%$.
Find the tax amount.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** Let $t$ be the tax amount.

$$\frac{t}{25}=\frac{4}{100}$$

Cross-multiplying gives $100t=25(4)=100$, so $t=1$. The tax amount is **$\$1.00$**.

$\square$

</details>

### Problem 2: Find a Tax Amount

**Problem.** A lamp has an original price of $\$72.00$. The tax rate is $7.5\%$. Find
the tax amount.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** Let $t$ be the tax amount. $\frac{t}{72}=\frac{7.5}{100}$, so
$100t=72(7.5)=540$. Thus $t=5.40$. The tax amount is **$\$5.40$**.

$\square$

</details>

### Problem 3: Find a Tax Rate

**Problem.** The tax amount on an art kit with an original price of $\$40.00$ is
$\$3.20$. Find the tax rate.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** Let $r$ be the tax rate.

$$\frac{3.20}{40}=\frac{r}{100}$$

Then $40r=3.20(100)=320$, so $r=8$. The tax rate is **$8\%$**.

$\square$

</details>

### Problem 5: Find an Original Price From Tax

**Problem.** The tax amount on a game controller is $\$4.50$ at a rate of $5\%$. Find

## Part B: Discount Proportions

### Problem 6: Find a Discount Amount

**Problem.** An item has an original price of $\$64.00$ and a discount rate of $15\%$.
Find the discount amount.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** Let $d$ be the discount amount.

$$\frac{d}{64}=\frac{15}{100}$$

Then $100d=64(15)=960$, so $d=9.60$. The discount amount is **$\$9.60$**.

$\square$

</details>

### Problem 9: Find an Original Price From a Discount

**Problem.** A discount amount is $\$18.00$ at a discount rate of $20\%$. Find the
original price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** Let $p$ be the original price. $\frac{18}{p}=\frac{20}{100}$, so
$20p=18(100)=1800$. Thus $p=90$. The original price is **$\$90.00$**.

$\square$

</details>

## Part C: Set Up and Explain

### Problem 10: Correct the Proportion

**Problem.** A student wants to find the tax rate when the tax is $\$5.25$ on an
original price of $\$75.00$. The student writes

$$\frac{75}{5.25}=\frac{r}{100}.$$

Explain why this is not the correct proportion. Write the correct proportion and find
the tax rate.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

**Solution:** The tax amount must be the numerator and the original price must be the
denominator. The correct proportion is

$$\frac{5.25}{75}=\frac{r}{100}.$$

Cross-multiplying gives $75r=5.25(100)=525$, so $r=7$. The tax rate is **$7\%$**.

$\square$

</details>
