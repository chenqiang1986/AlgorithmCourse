# Lesson 6.2: The Distributive Law With Numbers Only
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This lesson uses only numbers to prepare for the distributive property with letters in
Lesson 7. The task is to replace parentheses with an equivalent expression while preserving
the numbers already present. Do not calculate any numerical products or a final numerical
value.

## 1. One Factor Reaches Every Addend

Multiplication distributes across addition: a number outside parentheses multiplies every
addend inside.

$$6(8+3)=6 \times 8+6 \times 3$$

The right-hand expression has the same value, but its parentheses have been opened. Notice
that $6$, $8$, and $3$ were preserved; no product was evaluated.

## 2. Distributing Across a Difference

When a subtraction appears inside parentheses, use this sequence: first write it as
addition of a negative number, distribute, determine the sign of each product, and finally
write addition of a negative product as subtraction.

$$\begin{aligned}
4(9-5)
  &=4\bigl(9+(-5)\bigr)\\
  &=4 \times 9+4 \times (-5)\\
  &=4 \times 9+\bigl(-(4 \times 5)\bigr)\\
  &=4 \times 9-4 \times 5
\end{aligned}$$

The outside $4$ reaches both addends. We have opened the parentheses and handled the signs,
but have not calculated either product.

## 3. Negative Factors

The same rule works when the outside factor is negative:

$$(-3)(7+2)=(-3) \times 7+(-3) \times 2$$

No numerical product is calculated. The transformation simply makes two multiplications
visible.

## 4. Rules for This Class

For every practice problem:

1. Write an equivalent expression with the requested parentheses removed or inserted.
2. Use only numbers already present in the original expression. A subtraction may be written
   as addition of a negative number.
3. Use $\times$ between two numerical factors.
4. Do not multiply or add to find a final numerical answer.

## 5. Reading Example: Open Parentheses Without Calculating

**Example (Open Parentheses).** Rewrite $5(12-7)$ without the parentheses. Use no numbers
other than $5$, $12$, and $7$, and do not calculate.

**Solution:**

Write the subtraction as addition of a negative number. Then distribute, process the sign of
the second product, and change adding a negative back to subtraction:

$$\begin{aligned}
5(12-7)
  &=5\bigl(12+(-7)\bigr)\\
  &=5 \times 12+5 \times (-7)\\
  &=5 \times 12+\bigl(-(5 \times 7)\bigr)\\
  &=5 \times 12-5 \times 7
\end{aligned}$$

$\square$

## 10. Reading Example: Several Differences

**Example (Open Parentheses and Handle Signs).** Rewrite
$3(4-3)-5(2-3)$ without parentheses. Do not calculate any numerical products.

**Solution:**

First, write every subtraction as addition of a negative number. Then distribute each
outside factor:

$$\begin{aligned}
3(4-3)-5(2-3)
  &=3\bigl(4+(-3)\bigr)+(-5)\bigl(2+(-3)\bigr)\\
  &=3 \times 4+3 \times (-3)+(-5) \times 2+(-5) \times (-3)
\end{aligned}$$

Now process the sign of every product, still without calculating any product:

$$\begin{aligned}
3 \times 4+3 \times (-3)+(-5) \times 2+(-5) \times (-3)
  &=3 \times 4+\bigl(-(3 \times 3)\bigr)+\bigl(-(5 \times 2)\bigr)+5 \times 3\\
  &=3 \times 4-3 \times 3-5 \times 2+5 \times 3
\end{aligned}$$

$\square$

## 11. A Faster Way to Handle Signs

The full sequence above explains why the signs work. Once you understand it, you usually do
not need to write addition of negative numbers each time. Distribute to every term, then use
the signs already attached to the factors:

- positive times positive gives a positive term;
- positive times negative, or negative times positive, gives a negative term;
- negative times negative gives a positive term.

So, for example, you can go directly from

$$3(4-3)-5(2-3)$$

to

$$3 \times 4-3 \times 3-5 \times 2+5 \times 3,$$

as long as every term has received the outside factor. This is a shortcut for the same
sequence, not a reason to calculate the products.

## Key Takeaways

- An outside factor multiplies every addend inside parentheses.
- To explain signs fully: rewrite subtraction as addition of a negative, distribute, process
  product signs, then turn addition of a negative back into subtraction.
- After that process is understood, distribute directly and use the sign rules for each term.
- Equivalent forms can be written without evaluating any numerical products.
