# Homework: Lesson 6.3 — Fraction Multiplication, Division, and Reduction
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This homework practices [Lesson 6.3](./06.3-fraction-multiplication-division-reduction.md).
Leave products and sums in factor form (write $3\times5$, not $15$) and use only the numbers
already in each problem. Reduce wherever a factor cancels.

### Problem 1

Write as one fraction: $\dfrac{3}{7}\times\dfrac{5}{11}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{3\times5}{7\times11}$$

</details>

### Problem 2

Write as one fraction: $\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)\times\dfrac{7}{9}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$-\frac{2\times7}{5\times9}$$

</details>

### Problem 3

Write as one fraction: $\left(-\dfrac{4}{13}\right)\times\left(-\dfrac{6}{7}\right)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{4\times6}{13\times7}$$

</details>

### Problem 4

Write as one fraction: $\dfrac{9}{4}\div\dfrac{7}{5}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{9}{4}\times\frac{5}{7}=\frac{9\times5}{4\times7}$$

</details>

### Problem 5

Write as one fraction: $\dfrac{5}{6}\div\left(-\dfrac{11}{3}\right)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$-\frac{5\times3}{6\times11}$$

</details>

### Problem 6

Simplify: $\dfrac{8}{9}\times\dfrac{9}{13}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{8\times9}{9\times13}=\frac{8}{13}$$

</details>

### Problem 7

Simplify: $\dfrac{7}{10}\times\dfrac{4}{7}\times\dfrac{10}{13}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{7\times4\times10}{10\times7\times13}=\frac{4}{13}$$

</details>

### Problem 8

Simplify: $\dfrac{5}{12}\div\dfrac{5}{7}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{5}{12}\times\frac{7}{5}=\frac{5\times7}{12\times5}=\frac{7}{12}$$

</details>

### Problem 9

Simplify: $\dfrac{\quad\dfrac{3}{8}\quad}{\quad\dfrac{3}{11}\quad}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\frac{3}{8}\times\frac{11}{3}=\frac{3\times11}{8\times3}=\frac{11}{8}$$

</details>

### Problem 10

Simplify: $\left(-\dfrac{6}{11}\right)\times\dfrac{4}{5}\div\left(-\dfrac{6}{5}\right)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\left(-\frac{6}{11}\right)\times\frac{4}{5}\times\left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)
=\frac{6\times4\times5}{11\times5\times6}=\frac{4}{11}$$

</details>
