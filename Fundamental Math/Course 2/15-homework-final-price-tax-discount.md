# Homework: Lesson 15 — Final Price After Tax and Discount
*Fundamental Math / Course 2*

This homework accompanies [Lesson 15](./15-final-price-tax-discount.md). Write each
percent as a decimal before using a multiplier. Round money answers to the nearest cent.

Use these formulas when they fit the question:

$$\text{final price}=\text{original price}\times(1+\text{tax rate})$$

$$\text{sale price}=\text{original price}\times(1-\text{discount rate})$$

When an item is discounted and then taxed, find the sale price first. The tax rate
applies to the sale price.

## Part A: Final Price With Tax

### Problem 1

**Problem.** A $\$24.00$ notebook has a $5\%$ tax. Find the final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The tax rate is $0.05$. Thus

$$24.00(1+0.05)=24.00(1.05)=25.20.$$

The final price is **$\$25.20$**.

</details>

### Problem 2

**Problem.** A $\$68.00$ lamp has a $7.5\%$ tax. Find the final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The tax rate is $0.075$, so

$$68.00(1+0.075)=68.00(1.075)=73.10.$$

The final price is **$\$73.10$**.

</details>

### Problem 3

**Problem.** A final price of $\$96.30$ includes $7\%$ tax. Find the original,
pre-tax price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The final price is $107\%$, or $1.07$, times the original price. Let $p$ be the
original price.

$$1.07p=96.30$$

$$p=\frac{96.30}{1.07}=90.00.$$

The pre-tax price is **$\$90.00$**.

</details>

### Problem 4

**Problem.** A customer paid $\$42.80$ after $7\%$ tax. Find both the pre-tax price
and the tax amount.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Let $p$ be the pre-tax price. Since the final price is $1.07p$,

$$1.07p=42.80,$$

so $p=40.00$. The tax amount is $42.80-40.00=2.80$. The pre-tax price is
**$\$40.00$**, and the tax is **$\$2.80$**.

</details>

## Part B: Sale Price With a Discount

### Problem 5

**Problem.** A $\$75.00$ shirt is discounted $20\%$. Find the sale price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The discount rate is $0.20$. The sale-price multiplier is $1-0.20=0.80$.

$$75.00(0.80)=60.00.$$

The sale price is **$\$60.00$**.

</details>

### Problem 6

**Problem.** A $\$48.00$ game is discounted $12.5\%$. Find the sale price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The discount rate is $0.125$, so the sale-price multiplier is $0.875$.

$$48.00(0.875)=42.00.$$

The sale price is **$\$42.00$**.

</details>

### Problem 7

**Problem.** After a $25\%$ discount, a backpack costs $\$54.00$. Find the original
price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

A $25\%$ discount leaves $75\%$, or $0.75$, of the original price. Let $p$ be the
original price.

$$0.75p=54.00$$

$$p=\frac{54.00}{0.75}=72.00.$$

The original price is **$\$72.00$**.

</details>

### Problem 8

**Problem.** A sale price of $\$68.00$ is the result of a $15\%$ discount. Find the
original price and the discount amount.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

A $15\%$ discount leaves $85\%$, or $0.85$, of the original price. Let $p$ be the
original price.

$$0.85p=68.00$$

$$p=\frac{68.00}{0.85}=80.00.$$

The discount amount is $80.00-68.00=12.00$. The original price is **$\$80.00$**, and
the discount is **$\$12.00$**.

</details>

## Part C: Discount, Then Tax

### Problem 9

**Problem.** A $\$120.00$ bicycle helmet is discounted $25\%$. Then $8\%$ tax is
charged. Find the final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

First find the sale price:

$$120.00(1-0.25)=120.00(0.75)=90.00.$$

Tax is charged on $\$90.00$:

$$90.00(1+0.08)=90.00(1.08)=97.20.$$

The final price is **$\$97.20$**.

</details>

### Problem 10

**Problem.** A $\$50.00$ board game is $10\%$ off, then has $6\%$ tax. Find the
final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The sale price is

$$50.00(1-0.10)=50.00(0.90)=45.00.$$

Then add tax:

$$45.00(1+0.06)=45.00(1.06)=47.70.$$

The final price is **$\$47.70$**.

</details>

### Problem 11

**Problem.** A $\$160.00$ tablet is discounted $15\%$, then taxed $7.5\%$. Find the
sale price, tax amount, and final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The sale price is

$$160.00(1-0.15)=160.00(0.85)=136.00.$$

The tax amount is $0.075(136.00)=10.20$. Therefore the final price is

$$136.00+10.20=146.20.$$

The sale price is **$\$136.00$**, the tax is **$\$10.20$**, and the final price is
**$\$146.20$**.

</details>

### Problem 12: Explain the Error

**Problem.** A $\$100.00$ item is discounted $20\%$ and then taxed $10\%$. Jordan
calculates $100(1-0.20+0.10)=90$ and says the final price is $\$90.00$. Explain the
error and find the correct final price.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Jordan added the percent changes as though both were based on the original $\$100.00$.
The tax is charged after the discount, so it must be found from the sale price. First,
the sale price is

$$100.00(0.80)=80.00.$$

Then apply tax:

$$80.00(1.10)=88.00.$$

The correct final price is **$\$88.00$**.

</details>
