## Directions

- Show clear work. Unsupported answers may receive limited credit.

- There are **100 points** total. The point values are shown beside each problem.

## Part A: Integer Operations (20 points)

### Problem 1 (12 points)

Evaluate each expression.

1. $-17+24$
2. $-8-13$
3. $(-6)(-7)$
4. $45\div(-9)$

### Problem 2 (8 points)

Evaluate. Show the order of operations you use.

$$-4(3-8)+18\div(-3)$$

## Part B: Fraction Operations (20 points)

### Problem 3 (10 points)

Evaluate and reduce each answer to simplest form.

1. $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}\right)\left(\dfrac{10}{21}\right)$
2. $\dfrac{7}{12}\div\left(-\dfrac{14}{9}\right)$

### Problem 4 (6 points)

Evaluate and reduce:

$$-\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{7}{12}$$

### Problem 5 (4 points)

A hiker walked $\dfrac{3}{4}$ mile in the morning and $\dfrac{5}{6}$ mile in the
afternoon. How many miles did the hiker walk altogether? Show an equation and simplify
your answer.

## Part C: Expressions and Equations (32 points)

### Problem 6 (10 points)

1. Evaluate $-3x+8$ when $x=-4$. (4 points)
2. Simplify: $7a-5+3a+12$. (6 points)

### Problem 7 (8 points)

Use the distributive property, then combine like terms:

$$-3(2y-5)+4y-7$$

### Problem 8 (10 points)

Solve each equation. Check your answer by substitution.

1. $\dfrac{x}{-4}+6=1$ (4 points)
2. $5x-9=3x+7$ (6 points)

### Problem 9 (4 points)

A streaming service charges a one-time $\$12$ activation fee and $\$7$ per month. A
customer paid $\$54$ in all. Write and solve an equation to find the number of months
of service.

## Part D: One-Variable Inequalities (28 points)

### Problem 10 (6 points)

Solve and describe the number-line graph:

$$x-9> -2$$

### Problem 11 (8 points)

Solve and describe the number-line graph. Be sure to explain whether the inequality
symbol changes.

$$-5x+4\leq19$$

### Problem 12 (9 points)

Solve and describe the number-line graph:

$$3x-7\geq x+5$$

### Problem 13 (5 points)

The temperature $t$, in degrees Celsius, must be below $-3^\circ\mathrm{C}$ for a
school to cancel an outdoor activity. Write an inequality for every temperature that
cancels the activity, then describe its number-line graph.
