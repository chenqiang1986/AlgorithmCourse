## Part A: Integer Operations

### Problem 1 (12 points)

1. $-17+24=\boxed{7}$
2. $-8-13=-8+(-13)=\boxed{-21}$
3. $(-6)(-7)=\boxed{42}$
4. $45\div(-9)=\boxed{-5}$

Award 3 points per part: 1 point for a correct sign rule or setup and 2 points for the
correct result.

### Problem 2 (8 points)

Evaluate inside parentheses first, then multiply and divide before adding:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-4(3-8)+18\div(-3)
&=-4(-5)+(-6)\\
&=20-6\\
&=\boxed{14}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Award 2 points each for the parentheses, multiplication, division, and final sum.

## Part B: Fraction Operations

### Problem 3 (10 points)

1. The factors have opposite signs:

   $$
   \left(-\frac35\right)\left(\frac{10}{21}\right)
   =-\frac{30}{105}=\boxed{-\frac27}.
   $$

2. Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal:

   $$
   \frac7{12}\div\left(-\frac{14}{9}\right)
   =\frac7{12}\left(-\frac9{14}\right)
   =-\frac{63}{168}=\boxed{-\frac38}.
   $$

Award 5 points per part. In part 2, give 2 points for using the reciprocal, 2 for
correct multiplication and sign, and 1 for reducing.

### Problem 4 (6 points)

Use $24$ as a common denominator:

$$
-\frac58+\frac7{12}
=-\frac{15}{24}+\frac{14}{24}
=\boxed{-\frac1{24}}.
$$

Award 2 points for equivalent fractions, 2 for combining them, and 2 for the simplified
answer.

### Problem 5 (4 points)

$$
\frac34+\frac56
=\frac9{12}+\frac{10}{12}
=\frac{19}{12}
=\boxed{1\frac7{12}\text{ miles}}.
$$

Award 1 point for the addition equation, 2 for the common-denominator calculation, and 1
for the simplified result. $\frac{19}{12}$ miles is also acceptable.

## Part C: Expressions and Equations

### Problem 6 (10 points)

1. Substitute $-4$ for $x$:

   $$-3(-4)+8=12+8=\boxed{20}.$$

2. Combine like terms:

   $$7a-5+3a+12=(7a+3a)+(-5+12)=\boxed{10a+7}.$$

Award 4 points for part 1: 2 for substitution and 2 for evaluation. Award 6 points for
part 2: 3 for the variable terms and 3 for the constants and simplified expression.

### Problem 7 (8 points)

Distribute $-3$ to both terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-3(2y-5)+4y-7
&=-6y+15+4y-7\\
&=\boxed{-2y+8}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Award 4 points for correct distribution and 4 for combining like terms.

### Problem 8 (10 points)

1. Subtract $6$, then multiply by $-4$:

   $$
   \frac{x}{-4}+6=1
   \implies\frac{x}{-4}=-5
   \implies x=\boxed{20}.
   $$

   Check: $20/(-4)+6=-5+6=1$.

2. Put variable terms on one side:

   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   5x-9&=3x+7\\
   2x-9&=7\\
   2x&=16\\
   x&=\boxed{8}.
   \end{aligned}
   $$

   Check: $5(8)-9=31=3(8)+7$.

Award 4 points for part 1 and 6 points for part 2. Award up to 1 point per part for a
valid substitution check; otherwise award the remaining points for correct inverse
operations.

### Problem 9 (4 points)

Let $m$ be the number of months:

$$
12+7m=54
\implies7m=42
\implies m=\boxed{6}.
$$

The customer had $\boxed{6\text{ months}}$ of service. Award 2 points for a correct
equation and 2 points for solving it.

## Part D: One-Variable Inequalities

### Problem 10 (6 points)

$$x-9>-2\implies\boxed{x>7}.$$

The graph has an **open circle at $7$** and is shaded to the **right**. Award 3 points
for the solution and 3 for the graph description.

### Problem 11 (8 points)

Subtract $4$, then divide by $-5$. Dividing by a negative reverses the inequality:

$$
-5x+4\leq19
\implies-5x\leq15
\implies\boxed{x\geq-3}.
$$

The graph has a **closed circle at $-3$** and is shaded to the **right**. Award 3 points
for isolating $-5x$, 2 for flipping and solving, and 3 for the graph description.

### Problem 12 (9 points)

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x-7&\geq x+5\\
2x-7&\geq5\\
2x&\geq12\\
x&\geq\boxed{6}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The graph has a **closed circle at $6$** and is shaded to the **right**. Award 6 points
for solving and 3 for the graph description.

### Problem 13 (5 points)

“Below $-3^\circ\mathrm{C}$” does not include $-3^\circ\mathrm{C}$ itself:

$$\boxed{t<-3}.$$

The graph has an **open circle at $-3$** and is shaded to the **left**. Award 2 points
for the inequality, 1 for excluding the endpoint, and 2 for the graph direction.
