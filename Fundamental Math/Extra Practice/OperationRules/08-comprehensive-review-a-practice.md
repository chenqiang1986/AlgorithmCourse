A balanced review: one block of questions from each earlier lesson, from rewriting rules to fraction operations.

## Rules

This set reviews **Lessons 1–7**. Keep these rules handy:

1. **Subtraction ↔ Addition:** $a-b=a+(-b)$. **Commutative:** $a+b=b+a$.
2. **Distributive law:** $a(b+c)=ab+ac$ (and backwards to factor). A leading minus means multiplying by $-1$; $(-a)(-b)=ab$.
3. **Fractions, multiply:** $\dfrac{a}{b}\times\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{ac}{bd}$; cancel common factors first; reduce.
4. **Fractions, add:** common denominator first; convert mixed ↔ improper as needed.
5. **Fractions, divide:** Keep–Change–Flip; for a big fraction, simplify top and bottom, or multiply both by the LCD.

Part A is **rewrite only** (do not calculate). From Part B on, **calculate** and give a **reduced fraction**, or a **mixed number** if the answer is improper.
