# Practice: Comprehensive Challenge (Lessons 1–7)
*Fundamental Math / Extra Practice / OperationRules*

Multi-step problems: fractions with negatives, nested complex fractions, distributing with fractions, and word problems.

## Rules

This is the **challenge set** for Lessons 1–7. Every problem takes several steps. Show each step; do not skip.

- Rewriting rules: $a-b=a+(-b)$; distributive law (both directions); $(-a)(-b)=ab$.
- Fractions: multiply across and cancel; common denominator to add; Keep–Change–Flip to divide.
- Complex fractions: multiply top and bottom by the LCD, or work from the inside out.
- Word problems: write the operation you are using before you calculate; include units.

Parts A–E ask for **calculation**; Part F is **rewrite only** (do not combine terms).

**Part A**

1. $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}=1$
2. $\dfrac{5}{4}\times\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{5}{6}$
3. $2-2=0$
4. $\dfrac{1}{2}\div\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{3}{2}=1\,\dfrac{1}{2}$
5. $\dfrac{7}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2}=3$ ($\frac74\times\frac27=\frac12$)

**Part B**

6. $-\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{2}{4}=-\dfrac{1}{4}$
7. $\dfrac{4}{6}+\left(-\dfrac{5}{6}\right)=-\dfrac{1}{6}$
8. $\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{15}{4}=\dfrac{3}{2}=1\,\dfrac{1}{2}$
9. $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (two negatives give a positive)
10. $-\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{7}{3}=-\dfrac{9}{6}+\dfrac{14}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}$

**Part C**

11. $6x-9$
12. $-6a-4b+2$
13. $\dfrac{5}{6}x+\dfrac{5}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}x+1=\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{7}{2}=\dfrac{x+7}{2}$
14. $\dfrac{4}{9}(x+2y-3)$
15. $\dfrac{3}{5}(17+3)=\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot20=12$

**Part D**

16. $\dfrac{3}{4}\div\dfrac{3}{8}=2$
17. $\dfrac{3}{2}\div\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{9}{4}=2\,\dfrac{1}{4}$
18. $1+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2};\ \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\dfrac{2}{3};\ 1+\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{5}{3};\ \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{5}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{5}$
19. $\text{LCD}=ab:\ \dfrac{a^2-b^2}{b+a}=\dfrac{(a-b)(a+b)}{a+b}=a-b$ (for $a,b\neq0$, $a\neq-b$)
20. $\dfrac{y-x}{xy}\div(y-x)=\dfrac{1}{xy}$ (for $x\neq y$)

**Part E**

21. $\dfrac{3}{4}\times\dfrac{5}{2}=\dfrac{15}{8}=1\,\dfrac{7}{8}$ cups
22. $\dfrac{11}{2}\div\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{22}{3}=7\,\dfrac{1}{3}$, so **7 whole pieces**. They use $7\times\frac34=5\frac14$ m, so $\frac14$ m is left over.
23. $\dfrac{5}{3}+\dfrac{11}{4}=\dfrac{20}{12}+\dfrac{33}{12}=\dfrac{53}{12}=4\,\dfrac{5}{12}$ miles
24. $\dfrac{3}{5}\times\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=\dfrac{3}{5}\times\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the tank
25. Area $=\dfrac{9}{4}\times\dfrac{4}{3}=3$ m². Perimeter $=2\left(\dfrac{9}{4}+\dfrac{4}{3}\right)=2\cdot\dfrac{43}{12}=\dfrac{43}{6}=7\,\dfrac{1}{6}$ m.
26. $\dfrac{7}{2}\div7=\dfrac{1}{2}$ pizza

**Part F**

27. $5+(-2)(x+(-3))=5+(-2)x+2\times3=5-2x+2\times3$
28. $6a+6(-b)=6(a+(-b))=6(a-b)$
29. $(-1)(-x)+(-1)(2y)=x+(-2y)=x-2y$
30. $7-3a-3b-2c+2$ (the $-2(c-1)$ becomes $-2c+2$)
