# Practice: Fraction Multiplication and Reduction
*Fundamental Math / Extra Practice / OperationRules*

Multiply fractions and reduce the result. Part D teaches the shortcut: cancel first.

## Rules

1. **Multiply across:** $\dfrac{a}{b}\times\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{a\cdot c}{b\cdot d}$ (numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator)
2. **Whole number as a fraction:** $n=\dfrac{n}{1}$
3. **Reduce:** divide the numerator and denominator by the same number (best: their greatest common factor, GCF): $\dfrac{a\cdot k}{b\cdot k}=\dfrac{a}{b}$
4. **Cross-cancel first:** before multiplying, cancel any common factor between a *numerator* and the *denominator of a different fraction*. The numbers stay small and the answer is the same.
5. **Signs:** same signs give a positive product; different signs give a negative product.

Give every final answer as a **fully reduced fraction**. Variables are never $0$.
