# Practice: Fraction Division and Complex Fractions
*Fundamental Math / Extra Practice / OperationRules*

Divide fractions, then learn two strategies for simplifying complicated fractions (fractions inside fractions).

## Rules

1. **Reciprocal:** the reciprocal of $\dfrac{a}{b}$ is $\dfrac{b}{a}$ (a whole number $n$ has reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{n}$)
2. **Keep–Change–Flip:** $\dfrac{a}{b}\div\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{a}{b}\times\dfrac{d}{c}$, then cross-cancel and reduce
3. **Fraction bar means divide:** $\dfrac{\;\frac{a}{b}\;}{\frac{c}{d}}=\dfrac{a}{b}\div\dfrac{c}{d}$
4. **Mixed numbers:** convert to improper fractions before dividing
5. **Complex fractions with sums/differences, two strategies:**
   - **Strategy 1:** simplify the top and the bottom separately into single fractions, then divide.
   - **Strategy 2:** multiply the top and bottom by the **LCD** of every small fraction inside; all the small fractions disappear at once.
6. **Order:** multiplication and division go left to right.

Give final answers **reduced**. Variables are never $0$ (and never make a denominator $0$).
