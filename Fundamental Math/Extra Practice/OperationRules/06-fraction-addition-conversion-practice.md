# Practice: Fraction Addition and Conversions
*Fundamental Math / Extra Practice / OperationRules*

Add fractions with common denominators, and practice converting between mixed numbers and improper fractions, back and forth.

## Rules

1. **Same denominator:** $\dfrac{a}{c}+\dfrac{b}{c}=\dfrac{a+b}{c}$ (add the numerators, keep the denominator)
2. **Different denominators:** first rewrite both fractions with a common denominator (the LCD is best), using $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{a\cdot k}{b\cdot k}$
3. **Mixed number → improper fraction:** $w\,\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{w\cdot b+a}{b}$
4. **Improper fraction → mixed number:** divide the numerator by the denominator; the quotient is the whole part and the remainder is the new numerator
5. **Whole number as a fraction:** $n=\dfrac{n\cdot b}{b}$ for any $b\neq 0$

Give final answers **reduced**. When an answer is an improper fraction, also write it as a **mixed number**.
