# Course 1 Diagnostic Test
*Fundamental Math / Course 1*

This test samples every major 6th-grade topic to identify which skills a student already
has and which need review before starting Course 1, the standard-paced 6th-grade course
that follows the slower track of Middle School Placement.

## How to Use This Test

- Time limit: **40 minutes**. No calculator.
- Attempt every problem — if you're unsure, mark your best guess and move on rather than
  spending more than 2 minutes on any one question.
- This is a placement tool, not a graded exam. The answer key and full solutions live in
  [02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md](./02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md), together with a
  topic map so you can see exactly which skills to review.

## Problem 1: Ratio Language

A classroom has 12 boys and 18 girls. What is the ratio of boys to girls, in simplest form?

(A) 2:3  (B) 3:2  (C) 18:12  (D) 12:18  (E) 3:5

## Problem 2: Unit Rates

A recipe uses 5 cups of flour to make 20 cookies. What is the unit rate in cups of flour
per cookie?

(A) 4 cups/cookie  (B) 0.25 cups/cookie  (C) 5 cups/cookie  (D) 20 cups/cookie
(E) 0.5 cups/cookie

## Problem 3: Ratio Tables and Equivalent Ratios

A ratio table shows that 3 pencils cost \$2. At this rate, how much do 12 pencils cost?

(A) \$6  (B) \$4  (C) \$8  (D) \$24  (E) \$9

## Problem 4: Scaling Real-World Quantities

A recipe that serves 4 people calls for 6 cups of rice. How many cups of rice are needed
to scale the recipe to serve 10 people?

(A) 12  (B) 9  (C) 10  (D) 15  (E) 24

## Problem 5: Multi-Digit Division

Evaluate: $4536 \div 24$

(A) 198  (B) 168  (C) 201  (D) 176  (E) 189

## Problem 6: Dividing Fractions by Fractions

Evaluate: $\dfrac{3}{4} \div \dfrac{2}{5}$

(A) $\dfrac{15}{8}$  (B) $\dfrac{6}{20}$  (C) $\dfrac{8}{15}$  (D) $\dfrac{6}{5}$  (E) $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Problem 7: Ordering Rational Numbers

Which list orders the numbers $-3, 2, -5, 0, 4$ from least to greatest?

(A) $-3, -5, 0, 2, 4$  (B) $-5, -3, 0, 2, 4$  (C) $4, 2, 0, -3, -5$  (D) $-5, -3, 2, 0, 4$
(E) $0, -3, -5, 2, 4$

## Problem 8: Coordinate Plane Quadrants

In which quadrant does the point $(-4, 3)$ lie?

(A) Quadrant I  (B) Quadrant IV  (C) Quadrant II  (D) Quadrant III  (E) It lies on an axis

## Problem 9: Order of Operations With Exponents

Evaluate: $3^2 + 4 \times (7 - 3)$

(A) 28  (B) 19  (C) 22  (D) 25  (E) 49

## Problem 10: The Distributive Property

Use the distributive property to expand: $4(x + 5)$

(A) $4x + 5$  (B) $x + 20$  (C) $4x + 9$  (D) $9x$  (E) $4x + 20$

## Problem 11: One-Step Equations

Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{x}{6} = 9$

(A) 54  (B) 15  (C) 3  (D) 1.5  (E) 45

## Problem 12: One-Step Inequalities

Solve the inequality: $x + 7 \le 15$

(A) $x \le 22$  (B) $x \le 8$  (C) $x \ge 8$  (D) $x \le 7$  (E) $x \ge 22$

## Problem 13: Area of a Triangle

Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.

(A) 60 cm²  (B) 16 cm²  (C) 30 cm²  (D) 120 cm²  (E) 40 cm²

## Problem 14: Area of a Parallelogram

Find the area of a parallelogram with base 9 cm and height 4 cm.

(A) 13 cm²  (B) 18 cm²  (C) 26 cm²  (D) 36 cm²  (E) 45 cm²

## Problem 15: Volume of a Rectangular Prism

Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length $2\tfrac{1}{2}$ in, width 2 in, and
height 3 in.

(A) 12 in³  (B) 17.5 in³  (C) 7.5 in³  (D) 10 in³  (E) 15 in³

## Problem 16: Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

Find the surface area of a rectangular prism with length 5 cm, width 3 cm, and height 2 cm.

(A) 62 cm²  (B) 60 cm²  (C) 31 cm²  (D) 56 cm²  (E) 68 cm²

## Problem 17: Median

Find the median of the data set: 7, 2, 9, 4, 5

(A) 4  (B) 5  (C) 5.4  (D) 7  (E) 9

## Problem 18: Interquartile Range

Find the interquartile range (IQR) of the data set: 3, 7, 8, 12, 14, 18, 21

(A) 7  (B) 18  (C) 11  (D) 14  (E) 21

## Problem 19: Mode From a Dot Plot

A dot plot shows the number of pets owned by 10 students: 0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4.
What is the mode of this data set?

(A) 0  (B) 2  (C) 4  (D) 1  (E) 3

## Problem 20: Range From a Box Plot

A box plot has the five-number summary: minimum = 10, $Q_1$ = 15, median = 20, $Q_3$ = 28,
maximum = 35. What is the range of the data?

(A) 18  (B) 20  (C) 13  (D) 35  (E) 25
