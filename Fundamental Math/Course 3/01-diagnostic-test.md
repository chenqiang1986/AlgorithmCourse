# Course 3 Diagnostic Test
*Fundamental Math / Course 3*

This test samples every major pre-algebra topic to identify which skills a student already
has and which need review before starting Course 3, the bridge between Middle School
Placement and Algebra I.

## How to Use This Test

- Time limit: **40 minutes**. No calculator.
- Attempt every problem — if you're unsure, mark your best guess and move on rather than
  spending more than 2 minutes on any one question.
- This is a placement tool, not a graded exam. The answer key and full solutions live in
  [02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md](./02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md), together with a
  topic map so you can see exactly which skills to review.

## Problem 1: Order of Operations With Integers

Evaluate: $-3 + 4 \times (-2)^2 \div 8$

(A) -1  (B) -5  (C) 1  (D) 5  (E) 11

## Problem 2: Adding and Subtracting Integers

Evaluate: $-8 - (-5) + (-3)$

(A) -16  (B) -6  (C) 0  (D) 6  (E) -10

## Problem 3: Multiplying and Dividing Integers

Evaluate: $(-6) \times 4 \div (-3)$

(A) -8  (B) 2  (C) 8  (D) -2  (E) -72

## Problem 4: Adding and Subtracting Fractions

Evaluate: $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4}$

(A) $\dfrac{3}{7}$  (B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  (C) $\dfrac{5}{6}$  (D) $\dfrac{11}{12}$  (E) $\dfrac{11}{7}$

## Problem 5: Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

(A) $\dfrac{3}{10}$  (B) $\dfrac{8}{15}$  (C) $\dfrac{2}{3}$  (D) $\dfrac{5}{8}$  (E) $\dfrac{15}{8}$

## Problem 6: Simplifying Ratios

Simplify the ratio $18 : 24$ to lowest terms.

(A) $3:4$  (B) $4:3$  (C) $6:8$  (D) $9:12$  (E) $2:3$

## Problem 7: Solving Proportions

Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{x}{6} = \dfrac{10}{15}$

(A) 9  (B) 4  (C) 6  (D) 2.5  (E) 25

## Problem 8: Percent of a Number

What is 35% of 60?

(A) 35  (B) 25  (C) 21  (D) 17.5  (E) 2.1

## Problem 9: Percent Change

A shirt's price increases from \$40 to \$52. What is the percent increase?

(A) 12%  (B) 23%  (C) 130%  (D) 30%  (E) 20%

## Problem 10: Evaluating Algebraic Expressions

Evaluate $3a^2 - 2b$ for $a = -2$ and $b = 5$.

(A) 22  (B) -2  (C) -22  (D) 32  (E) 2

## Problem 11: Combining Like Terms

Simplify: $-4x + 9 + 6x - 2$

(A) $2x + 7$  (B) $10x + 7$  (C) $2x + 11$  (D) $-2x + 7$  (E) $10x - 11$

## Problem 12: One-Step Equations

Solve for $x$: $-5x = 35$

(A) 7  (B) -7  (C) -30  (D) 40  (E) 30

## Problem 13: Two-Step Equations

Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{x}{3} + 4 = 10$

(A) 2  (B) 42  (C) 18  (D) 6  (E) 30

## Problem 14: Exponents

Evaluate: $2^4 + 3^2$

(A) 17  (B) 19  (C) 30  (D) 25  (E) 14

## Problem 15: Square Roots of Perfect Squares

(A) 17  (B) -7  (C) 19  (D) 2  (E) 7

## Problem 16: Pythagorean Theorem

A right triangle has legs of length 6 and 8. Find the length of the hypotenuse.

(A) 10  (B) 14  (C) 48  (D) 7  (E) 100

## Problem 17: Area of a Triangle

Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.

(A) 60 cm²  (B) 30 cm²  (C) 16 cm²  (D) 15 cm²  (E) 8 cm²

## Problem 18: Volume of a Rectangular Prism

A rectangular prism has length 4 cm, width 3 cm, and height 5 cm. Find its volume.

(A) 12 cm³  (B) 24 cm³  (C) 60 cm³  (D) 47 cm³  (E) 120 cm³

## Problem 19: Mean, Median, Mode, and Range

(A) 7  (B) 5  (C) 3  (D) 6  (E) 6.5

(A) Quadrant I  (B) Quadrant III  (C) Quadrant IV  (D) It lies on an axis  (E) Quadrant II
