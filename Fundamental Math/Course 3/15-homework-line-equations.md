## Part A: Write the Equation of a Line

### Problem 1

Write an equation for the line through $(5,-3)$ and $(5,8)$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Both points have $x=5$, so the line is vertical: $\boxed{x=5}$.

</details>

### Problem 2

Write $3x+2y=14$ in slope-intercept form. State the slope and the $y$-intercept.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$2y=-3x+14\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=-\frac32x+7.$$

Thus the equation is $\boxed{y=-\frac32x+7}$, its slope is $\boxed{-\frac32}$, and
its $y$-intercept is $\boxed{(0,7)}$.

</details>

## Part B: Intersections of Lines

After finding $x$, substitute back to find $y$, then check in the other equation.

### Problem 3

Find the intersection of $y=2x-5$ and $y=-x+4$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$2x-5=-x+4\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3x=9\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=3.$$

Then $y=2(3)-5=1$, so the intersection is $\boxed{(3,1)}$.

</details>

### Problem 4

Find the intersection of $x=-2$ and $y=4x+9$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Substitute $x=-2$ into the second equation: $y=4(-2)+9=1$. The intersection is
$\boxed{(-2,1)}$.

</details>

### Problem 5

How many intersection points do $y=-3x+2$ and $6x+2y=4$ have? Explain.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$6x+2y=4\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3x+y=2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=-3x+2.$$

They are the same line, so there are $\boxed{\text{infinitely many intersections}}$.

</details>

### Problem 6

How many intersection points do $y=\frac12x-1$ and $x-2y=8$ have? Explain.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$x-2y=8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=\frac12x-4.$$

The slopes are both $\frac12$, but the $y$-intercepts differ, so the lines are parallel
and have $\boxed{\text{no intersection}}$.

</details>

## Part C: Word Problems

Define the variables before writing each equation. Include units in your interpretation.

### Problem 7: A Constant Rate

A streaming service charges a $12$ dollar sign-up fee plus $7 per month. Let $m$ be the
number of months and let $C$ be the total cost in dollars.

1. Write an equation for $C$ in terms of $m$.
2. Find the total cost after $5$ months.
3. Interpret the slope and the $C$-intercept.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\boxed{C=7m+12}.$$

After $5$ months, $C=7(5)+12=\boxed{47}$, so the cost is $\boxed{\$47}$. The slope
means the cost rises by $\$7$ per month, and the $C$-intercept is the $\$12$ sign-up fee.

</details>

### Problem 8: When Two Plans Match

Gym A charges a $25$ dollar sign-up fee and $18 per month. Gym B charges a $55$ dollar
sign-up fee and $12 per month. After how many months do the plans have the same total
cost, and what is that cost?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Let $m$ be months and $C$ be total cost:

$$C=18m+25 \qquad\text{and}\qquad C=12m+55.$$

$$18m+25=12m+55\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6m=30\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=5.$$

At $5$ months, $C=18(5)+25=115$. The plans match at $\boxed{(5,115)}$: after
$\boxed{5\text{ months}}$, both cost $\boxed{\$115}$.

</details>

### Problem 9: Build a Model From Two Data Points

A water tank contains $120$ liters after $4$ minutes and $210$ liters after $10$ minutes.
Assume it fills at a constant rate.

1. Find the filling rate.
2. Write an equation for the amount $W$ of water after $t$ minutes.
3. How much water was in the tank at $t=0$?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$\text{rate}=\frac{210-120}{10-4}=\frac{90}{6}=15.$$

The tank fills at $\boxed{15\text{ liters per minute}}$. Using $(4,120)$,

$$W-120=15(t-4)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{W=15t+60}.$$

At $t=0$, the tank contained $\boxed{60\text{ liters}}$.

</details>
