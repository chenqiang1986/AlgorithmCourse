## Part A: Real Numbers, Decimals, and Exponents (28 points)

### Problem 1 (6 points)

Classify each number as **rational** or **irrational**. Give a brief reason for each.

1. $\sqrt{81}$
2. $-\dfrac{7}{12}$
3. $0.\overline{36}$
4. $\sqrt{18}$

### Problem 2 (6 points)

Write each number in the requested form. Show enough work to make the conversion clear.

1. Write $\dfrac{7}{40}$ as a decimal.
2. Write $0.1\overline{6}$ as a fraction in lowest terms.

### Problem 3 (8 points)

Simplify each expression. Write answers with positive exponents only.

1. $\dfrac{(2a^3b^{-2})^2}{4ab^{-1}}$
2. $81^{\frac34}$

### Problem 4 (8 points)

Evaluate and simplify:

$$
\frac{3^0+2^{-2}}{\left(\frac14\right)^{-1}}.
$$

## Part B: Scientific Notation (12 points)

### Problem 5 (5 points)

Write $0.000728$ in scientific notation.

### Problem 6 (7 points)

Compute and write the result in proper scientific notation:

$$
(6.4\times10^5)+(3.7\times10^4).
$$

## Part C: Single-Variable Linear Equations (20 points)

### Problem 7 (8 points)

Solve for $x$:

$$
4(2x-3)-5=3(x+4)+2x.
$$

### Problem 8 (7 points)

Classify the equation as having **one solution**, **no solution**, or **infinitely many
solutions**. Justify your classification.

$$
5(2x-1)-3x=7x-5.
$$

### Problem 9 (5 points)

The equation

$$
(k-2)x+5=3x-8
$$

has no solution. What is the value of $k$? Show how you know.

## Part D: Systems of Linear Equations (28 points)

### Problem 10 (8 points)

Solve the system for $(x,y)$:

$$
\begin{cases}
y=3x-8\\
2x+y=12
\end{cases}
$$

### Problem 11 (8 points)

Solve the system for $(x,y)$:

$$
\begin{cases}
3x+2y=7\\
4x-3y=32
\end{cases}
$$

### Problem 12 (12 points)

Solve the system for $(x,y)$:

$$
\begin{cases}
5x-2y=2\\
3x+4y=22
\end{cases}
$$

## Part E: Line Equations on the Coordinate Plane (24 points)

### Problem 13 (8 points)

Find the slope of the line through $(-2,5)$ and $(4,-4)$. Then write its equation in
slope-intercept form.

### Problem 14 (7 points)

For the line $2x+3y=12$:

1. Find the $x$- and $y$-intercepts as ordered pairs.
2. Rewrite the equation in slope-intercept form.

### Problem 15 (9 points)

A bike rental shop charges a one-time $\$18$ setup fee and $\$7$ per hour. Let $h$ be the
number of hours rented and let $C$ be the total cost in dollars.

1. Write an equation relating $C$ and $h$.
2. Find the cost of a 5-hour rental.
3. Explain the meaning of the slope and the $C$-intercept in this situation.
