## Part A: Ordered Pairs and Tables

### Problem 1

Which of these points lie on $y=3x-4$: $(0,-4)$, $(2,2)$, $(2,3)$, and $(-1,-7)$?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

For $x=0$, $y=3(0)-4=-4$, so $(0,-4)$ lies on the line. For $x=2$, $y=3(2)-4=2$, so
$(2,2)$ lies on it and $(2,3)$ does not. For $x=-1$, $y=3(-1)-4=-7$, so $(-1,-7)$ lies on
it.

The points are **$(0,-4)$, $(2,2)$, and $(-1,-7)$**.

</details>

### Problem 2

Complete a table of four points for $y=-2x+5$ using $x=-1,0,1,3$, then graph the line.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

| $x$ | $y=-2x+5$ | point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $-1$ | $7$ | $(-1,7)$ |
| $0$ | $5$ | $(0,5)$ |
| $1$ | $3$ | $(1,3)$ |
| $3$ | $-1$ | $(3,-1)$ |

Plot the four points and draw one straight line through them.

</details>

### Problem 3

Write the equation of the horizontal line through $(0,6)$ and $(5,6)$. State its slope.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Both points have $y=6$, so the line is $\boxed{y=6}$. Its slope is $0$.

</details>

## Part B: Read and Graph Slope-Intercept Form

### Problem 4

For $y=\frac{3}{2}x-4$, state the slope and $y$-intercept. Starting at the intercept, give
two more points on the line.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The slope is $\frac{3}{2}$ and the $y$-intercept is $(0,-4)$. From $(0,-4)$, move right $2$
and up $3$ to get $(2,-1)$. Repeat to get $(4,2)$.

</details>

### Problem 5

Graph $y=-\frac{1}{3}x+2$. Give its $x$-intercept as an ordered pair.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Plot the $y$-intercept $(0,2)$. The slope $-\frac13$ means right $3$, down $1$, producing
$(3,1)$ and then $(6,0)$. Thus the $x$-intercept is $\boxed{(6,0)}$.

</details>

### Problem 6

Find the slope of the line through $(-2,1)$ and $(4,-2)$. Then write its equation in
slope-intercept form.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$m=\frac{-2-1}{4-(-2)}=\frac{-3}{6}=-\frac12.$$

Use $(4,-2)$ in $y=mx+b$:

$$-2=-\frac12(4)+b=-2+b,$$

so $b=0$. The equation is $\boxed{y=-\frac12x}$.

</details>

### Problem 7

The line through $(1,5)$ and $(3,9)$ is shown by those two points. Write its equation in
slope-intercept form.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$m=\frac{9-5}{3-1}=\frac42=2.$$

Using $(1,5)$: $5=2(1)+b$, so $b=3$. The equation is $\boxed{y=2x+3}$.

</details>

## Part C: Standard Form and Special Lines

### Problem 8

Graph $3x+y=6$ by finding both intercepts. Then write it in slope-intercept form.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Set $x=0$: $y=6$, so the $y$-intercept is $(0,6)$. Set $y=0$: $3x=6$, so the
$x$-intercept is $(2,0)$. Draw the line through these points.

Solving for $y$ gives $\boxed{y=-3x+6}$.

</details>

### Problem 9

Write the equation of the vertical line through $(-4,2)$ and $(-4,-5)$. Explain why it has
no slope-intercept form.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Every point has $x=-4$, so the equation is $\boxed{x=-4}$. Its slope would require division
by the run $-4-(-4)=0$, so the slope is undefined. Therefore it cannot be written as
$y=mx+b$.

</details>

### Problem 10

For each equation, decide whether it represents a horizontal line, a vertical line, or
neither: (a) $y=-7$; (b) $x=3$; (c) $y=4x+1$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

(a) is horizontal because $y$ stays $-7$. (b) is vertical because $x$ stays $3$. (c) is
neither: it has nonzero slope $4$.

</details>

## Part D: Interpret a Linear Model

### Problem 11

A streaming service charges a $12$-dollar monthly fee plus $3$ dollars for each movie
rental. Let $x$ be the number of rentals and $y$ the monthly cost.

1. Write an equation for the situation.
2. Find the cost for $5$ rentals.
3. Explain what the slope and $y$-intercept mean in context.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The starting cost is $12$ and each rental adds $3$, so $\boxed{y=3x+12}$.

For $5$ rentals, $y=3(5)+12=27$, so the cost is **$\$27**.

The slope $3$ means $\$3 per rental. The $y$-intercept $(0,12)$ means the cost is $\$12$
with zero rentals.

</details>

### Problem 12

Two points on a line are $(0,-1)$ and $(6,2)$. Is $(10,4)$ on the same line? Show enough
work to justify your answer.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

The slope is

$$m=\frac{2-(-1)}{6-0}=\frac36=\frac12.$$

Since $(0,-1)$ is the $y$-intercept, the equation is $y=\frac12x-1$. At $x=10$, this gives
$y=\frac12(10)-1=4$. Therefore **$(10,4)$ is on the line**.

</details>
