## Full Solutions

### Problem 1: Order of Operations With Integers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Exponent first, then multiplication/division left to right, then addition:

$$-3 + 4 \times (-2)^2 \div 8 = -3 + 4 \times 4 \div 8 = -3 + 16 \div 8 = -3 + 2 = -1$$

The answer is **(A) -1**.

</details>

### Problem 2: Adding and Subtracting Integers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding its positive:

$$-8 - (-5) + (-3) = -8 + 5 - 3 = -6$$

The answer is **(B) -6**.

</details>

### Problem 3: Multiplying and Dividing Integers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Work left to right. A negative times a positive is negative, and a negative divided by a
negative is positive:

$$(-6) \times 4 \div (-3) = -24 \div (-3) = 8$$

The answer is **(C) 8**.

</details>

### Problem 4: Adding and Subtracting Fractions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rewrite both fractions with a common denominator of 12:

$$\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$$

The answer is **(D) $\dfrac{11}{12}$**.

</details>

### Problem 5: Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal:

$$\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{15}{8}$$

The answer is **(E) $\dfrac{15}{8}$**.

</details>

### Problem 6: Simplifying Ratios

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide both terms by their greatest common factor, 6:

$$18 : 24 = (18 \div 6) : (24 \div 6) = 3 : 4$$

The answer is **(A) $3:4$**.

</details>

### Problem 7: Solving Proportions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Cross-multiply, then solve for $x$:

$$\frac{x}{6} = \frac{10}{15} \implies 15x = 60 \implies x = 4$$

The answer is **(B) 4**.

</details>

### Problem 8: Percent of a Number

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Convert 35% to the decimal 0.35 and multiply:

$$0.35 \times 60 = 21$$

The answer is **(C) 21**.

</details>

### Problem 9: Percent Change

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The change is $52 - 40 = 12$. Percent change is the change divided by the **original**
value:

$$\frac{12}{40} = 0.30 = 30\%$$

The answer is **(D) 30%**.

</details>

### Problem 10: Evaluating Algebraic Expressions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Substitute $a = -2$ and $b = 5$. Note that $a^2 = (-2)^2 = 4$ is positive because the
negative is squared:

$$3a^2 - 2b = 3(4) - 2(5) = 12 - 10 = 2$$

The answer is **(E) 2**.

</details>

### Problem 11: Combining Like Terms

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Group the $x$ terms and the constants separately:

$$-4x + 9 + 6x - 2 = (-4x + 6x) + (9 - 2) = 2x + 7$$

The answer is **(A) $2x+7$**.

</details>

### Problem 12: One-Step Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide both sides by $-5$:

$$-5x = 35 \implies x = \frac{35}{-5} = -7$$

The answer is **(B) -7**.

</details>

### Problem 13: Two-Step Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Subtract 4 from both sides, then multiply both sides by 3:

$$\frac{x}{3} + 4 = 10 \implies \frac{x}{3} = 6 \implies x = 18$$

The answer is **(C) 18**.

</details>

### Problem 14: Exponents

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Evaluate each power before adding:

$$2^4 + 3^2 = 16 + 9 = 25$$

The answer is **(D) 25**.

</details>

### Problem 15: Square Roots of Perfect Squares

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Evaluate each square root, then subtract:

$$\sqrt{144} - \sqrt{25} = 12 - 5 = 7$$

The answer is **(E) 7**.

</details>

### Problem 16: Pythagorean Theorem

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

For legs $a = 6$ and $b = 8$, the hypotenuse $c$ satisfies $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$:

$$c = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10$$

The answer is **(A) 10**.

</details>

### Problem 17: Area of a Triangle

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The area of a triangle is half the base times the height:

$$A = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 6 = 30 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(B) 30 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 18: Volume of a Rectangular Prism

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Volume is length times width times height:

$$V = 4 \times 3 \times 5 = 60 \text{ cm}^3$$

The answer is **(C) 60 cm³**.

</details>

### Problem 19: Mean, Median, Mode, and Range

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Sort the data first: $3, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12$. With six values, the median is the average of the
two middle values:

$$\text{median} = \frac{5 + 7}{2} = 6$$

(The mean, $\frac{3+3+5+7+9+12}{6} = 6.5$, is a different statistic — don't mix them up.)

The answer is **(D) 6**.

</details>

### Problem 20: Coordinate Plane Quadrants

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Quadrant I has $(+,+)$, Quadrant II has $(-,+)$, Quadrant III has $(-,-)$, and Quadrant IV
has $(+,-)$. The point $(-4, 7)$ has a negative $x$-coordinate and a positive
$y$-coordinate:

$$(-4, 7) \implies (-, +) \implies \text{Quadrant II}$$

The answer is **(E) Quadrant II**.

</details>
