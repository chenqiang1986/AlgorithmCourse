# Middle School Placement
*Fundamental Math / Middle School Placement*

This folder contains the placement test used to sort an incoming 6th grader — a student
finishing 5th grade — onto the **faster track** (accelerated pathway starting with
[Course 3](../Course%203)) or the **slower track** (standard-paced 6th grade math) for
middle school.

## Course Goals

The placement test samples the full range of 5th-grade math skills, plus two
above-grade-level questions that specifically test readiness for acceleration:

1. Whole-number fluency: multi-digit multiplication and long division.
2. Decimal operations: adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing decimals.
3. Fraction operations: adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing fractions.
4. Measurement and geometry: area, perimeter, volume, and the coordinate plane.
5. Data and reasoning: line plots, multi-step word problems, and numerical patterns.
6. Early algebraic reasoning (challenge questions) that predict comfort with the pace of
   [Course 3](../Course%203).
