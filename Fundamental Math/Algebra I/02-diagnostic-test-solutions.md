## Full Solutions

### Problem 1: Order of Operations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Parentheses first, then the exponent, then multiplication/division left to right, then addition:

$$7 + 3(5-2)^2 \div 3 = 7 + 3(3)^2 \div 3 = 7 + 3(9) \div 3 = 7 + 27 \div 3 = 7 + 9 = 16$$

The answer is **(B) 16**.

</details>

### Problem 2: Combining Like Terms

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Group the $x$ terms and the constants separately:

$$4x + 7 - 2x + 3 = (4x - 2x) + (7 + 3) = 2x + 10$$

The answer is **(A) $2x + 10$**.

</details>

### Problem 3: One-Step / Two-Step Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$3x - 5 = 16 \implies 3x = 21 \implies x = 7$$

The answer is **(B) 7**.

</details>

### Problem 4: Variables on Both Sides

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Distribute the left side, then collect $x$ terms on one side:

$$2(x+3) = 5x - 6 \implies 2x + 6 = 5x - 6 \implies 12 = 3x \implies x = 4$$

The answer is **(C) 4**.

</details>

### Problem 5: Translating Word Problems

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

"Five less than twice a number" translates to $2n - 5$:

$$2n - 5 = 13 \implies 2n = 18 \implies n = 9$$

The answer is **(C) 9**.

</details>

### Problem 6: Solving Inequalities

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$3x + 4 < 19 \implies 3x < 15 \implies x < 5$$

The inequality symbol does not flip because both sides are only ever divided by a positive number.

The answer is **(A) $x < 5$**.

</details>

### Problem 7: Exponent Rules — Product

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents:

$$x^3 \cdot x^5 = x^{3+5} = x^8$$

The answer is **(A) $x^8$**.

</details>

### Problem 8: Exponent Rules — Power of a Power

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Raise both the coefficient and the variable factor to the outer power:

$$(2x^2)^3 = 2^3 \cdot (x^2)^3 = 8x^6$$

The answer is **(D) $8x^6$**.

</details>

### Problem 9: Exponent Rules — Quotient and Negative Exponents

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the exponents:

$$\frac{x^4}{x^7} = x^{4-7} = x^{-3} = \frac{1}{x^3}$$

The answer is **(B) $\dfrac{1}{x^3}$**.

</details>

### Problem 10: Adding Polynomials

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Combine like terms across the two polynomials:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3x^2 - 2x + 5) + (x^2 + 4x - 7) &= (3x^2 + x^2) + (-2x + 4x) + (5 - 7) \\
&= 4x^2 + 2x - 2
\end{aligned}
$$

The answer is **(A) $4x^2 + 2x - 2$**.

</details>

### Problem 11: Multiplying Binomials

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Use FOIL:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x+3)(x-5) &= x^2 - 5x + 3x - 15 \\
&= x^2 - 2x - 15
\end{aligned}
$$

The answer is **(A) $x^2 - 2x - 15$**.

</details>

### Problem 12: Factoring a Trinomial

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Find two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7: 3 and 4.

$$x^2 + 7x + 12 = (x+3)(x+4)$$

The answer is **(A) $(x+3)(x+4)$**.

</details>

### Problem 13: Factoring a Difference of Squares

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$x^2 - 49$ is a difference of squares, $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$, with $a = x$ and $b = 7$:

$$x^2 - 49 = (x-7)(x+7)$$

The answer is **(B) $(x-7)(x+7)$**.

</details>

### Problem 14: Simplifying Radicals

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Pull out the largest perfect-square factor of 72, which is 36:

$$\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{36}\sqrt{2} = 6\sqrt{2}$$

The answer is **(A) $6\sqrt{2}$**.

</details>

### Problem 15: Slope From Two Points

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{11 - 3}{6 - 2} = \frac{8}{4} = 2$$

The answer is **(A) 2**.

</details>

### Problem 16: Equation of a Line

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Use point-slope form with $m = -2$ and $(1, 5)$, then solve for $y$:

$$y - 5 = -2(x - 1) \implies y - 5 = -2x + 2 \implies y = -2x + 7$$

The answer is **(A) $y = -2x + 7$**.

</details>

### Problem 17: Evaluating a Function

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Substitute $x = -2$ into $f(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$:

$$f(-2) = 2(-2)^2 - 3(-2) + 1 = 2(4) + 6 + 1 = 8 + 6 + 1 = 15$$

The answer is **(A) 15**.

</details>

### Problem 18: Solving a System of Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Add the two equations to eliminate $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + y &= 10 \\
x - y &= 2 \\
\hline
2x &= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

So $x = 6$, and substituting back gives $y = 10 - 6 = 4$.

The answer is **(A) $(6, 4)$**.

</details>

### Problem 19: Direct Proportion Word Problem

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The rate is $150 \div 3 = 50$ miles per hour. In 7 hours:

$$50 \times 7 = 350$$

The answer is **(B) 350**.

</details>

### Problem 20: Applied System of Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $a$ be the number of adult tickets and $c$ the number of child tickets:

$$
\begin{aligned}
a + c &= 100 \\
8a + 5c &= 650
\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute $c = 100 - a$ into the second equation:

$$8a + 5(100 - a) = 650 \implies 8a + 500 - 5a = 650 \implies 3a = 150 \implies a = 50$$

The answer is **(B) 50**.

</details>
