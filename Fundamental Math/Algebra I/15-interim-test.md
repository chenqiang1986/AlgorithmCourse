## Directions

- Time limit: **60 minutes**. No calculator.
- Show your work. Label graph features and give exact answers; do not round radicals.
- You may use either substitution or elimination unless a method is specified.
- The test is worth **100 points**. Point values appear beside each problem.

## Part A: Systems of Linear Equations (34 points; about 20 minutes)

### Problem 1 (8 points)

Solve the system for $(x,y)$:

$$
\begin{cases}
3x+2y=11,\\
x-y=2.
\end{cases}
$$

### Problem 2 (10 points)

Solve the system for $(x,y,z)$. Show how you reduce it to a two-variable system.

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y+z=6,\\
2x-y+z=3,\\
x+2y-z=2.
\end{cases}
$$

### Problem 3 (8 points)

Consider

$$
\begin{cases}
2x+3y=7,\\
4x+ky=c.
\end{cases}
$$

1. Find $k$ and $c$ so the system has infinitely many solutions. (4 points)
2. If $c=15$, find $k$ so the system has no solution. Explain why. (4 points)

### Problem 4 (8 points)

For the homogeneous system below, calculate the determinant of its coefficient matrix.
Use the result to classify the number of solutions, then give all solutions in terms of
one parameter.

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y+z=0,\\
x-y=0,\\
2x+z=0.
\end{cases}
$$

## Part B: Lines and Distance (16 points; about 10 minutes)

### Problem 5 (8 points)

1. Find the equation of the line through $(-2,1)$ and $(2,3)$ in slope-intercept form.
   (4 points)
2. Find the equation of the line through $(2,-1)$ perpendicular to that line. (4 points)

### Problem 6 (8 points)

For the line $3x+4y=12$:

1. Give both intercepts as ordered pairs. (4 points)
2. Find the exact distance from $(0,0)$ to the line. (4 points)

## Part C: Inequalities and Linear Programming (20 points; about 12 minutes)

### Problem 7 (12 points)

Graph the feasible region for the system below. Draw and label each boundary, shade the
overlap, and list every vertex of the feasible region.

$$
\begin{cases}
x\ge 0,\\
y\ge 0,\\
x+y\le 6,\\
2x+y\le 8.
\end{cases}
$$

### Problem 8 (8 points)

Using the feasible region in Problem 7, find the maximum value of $P=3x+2y$ and the
point where it occurs. Show the value of $P$ at every vertex.

## Part D: Quadratics (30 points; about 18 minutes)

### Problem 9 (10 points)

Solve each equation over the real numbers. Use square roots for (1) and complete the
square for (2).

1. $(x-3)^2=12$ (4 points)
2. $x^2-6x+5=0$ (6 points)

### Problem 10 (10 points)

For $y=x^2-4x+3$, rewrite the equation in vertex form. State the vertex, axis of
symmetry, opening direction, $x$-intercepts, and $y$-intercept. Sketch a labeled graph.

### Problem 11 (10 points)

Factor and solve each equation. Show the zero-product step.

1. $x^2-9=0$ (3 points)
2. $x^2+5x=0$ (3 points)
3. $2x^2-5x-3=0$ (4 points)
