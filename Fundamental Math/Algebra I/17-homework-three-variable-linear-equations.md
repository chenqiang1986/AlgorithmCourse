# Homework: Lesson 17 — Three-Variable Linear Equations Review
*Fundamental Math / Algebra I*

Solve each system by elimination. Show the two helper equations created by eliminating
the same variable from two different pairs, write your final answer as $(x,y,z)$, and
check it in all three original equations.

## Problem 1

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y+z=4\\
2x-y+z=8\\
x+2y-z=-3
\end{cases}
$$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Using $(2)-2(1)$ and $(3)-(1)$ gives $3y+z=0$ and $y-2z=-7$.
Solving gives $z=3$ and $y=-1$. Then (1) gives $x=2$.
The solution is $\boxed{(2,-1,3)}$.

$\square$

</details>

## Problem 2

$$
\begin{cases}
2x+y+z=3\\
x-3y+2z=-7\\
3x+2y-z=8
\end{cases}
$$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Eliminating $z$ with $2(1)-(2)$ and $(1)+(3)$ gives
$3x+5y=13$ and $5x+3y=11$. Thus $x=1$ and $y=2$.
Substitution into (1) gives $z=-1$.
The solution is $\boxed{(1,2,-1)}$.

$\square$

</details>

## Problem 3

$$
\begin{cases}
2x+y+z=5\\
x-3y+2z=11\\
3x+2y-z=4
\end{cases}
$$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Eliminate $z$ using $2(1)-(2)$ and $(1)+(3)$:

$$
3x+5y=-1,\qquad 5x+3y=9.
$$

Solving gives $x=3$ and $y=-2$. From the first equation, $z=1$.
The solution is $\boxed{(3,-2,1)}$.

$\square$

</details>
