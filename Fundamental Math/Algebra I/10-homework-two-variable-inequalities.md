## Problem 1: Boundary and Shading

Graph $4x-2y\ge8$. State the boundary's equation and style, which side to shade, and one
point in the solution region.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

$$-2y\ge8-4x\implies y\le2x-4.$$

The boundary is $y=2x-4$, drawn **solid** because equality is included. Shade below it.
For example, $(0,-5)$ works because $4(0)-2(-5)=10\ge8$.

</details>

## Problem 2: Feasible Region and Vertices

Graph the feasible region and list all its vertices:

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y\le9\\
2x+y\le12\\
x\ge0,\ y\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The axis vertices are $(0,0)$, $(6,0)$, and $(0,9)$. Solve the diagonal boundaries:

$$x+y=9,\qquad2x+y=12 \implies x=3,\ y=6.$$

Thus the vertices are $\boxed{(0,0),\ (6,0),\ (3,6),\ (0,9)}$.

</details>

## Problem 3: Classify the Region

Decide whether this system has a bounded region, an unbounded region, or no solution.
Justify your answer with inequalities or a graph.

$$
\begin{cases}
y>x+2\\
y\le x-1\\
x\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The first condition requires $y>x+2$, but the second requires $y\le x-1$. Since
$x+2>x-1$ for every $x$, no $y$ can satisfy both. The system has
$\boxed{\text{no solution}}$.

</details>
