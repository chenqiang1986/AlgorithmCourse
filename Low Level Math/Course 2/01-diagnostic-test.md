# Course 2 Diagnostic Test

This test samples every major 7th-grade topic to identify which skills a student already
has and which need review before starting Course 2, the bridge between Middle School
Placement and Course 3.

## How to Use This Test

- Time limit: **40 minutes**. No calculator.
- Attempt every problem — if you're unsure, mark your best guess and move on rather than
  spending more than 2 minutes on any one question.
- This is a placement tool, not a graded exam. The answer key and full solutions live in
  [02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md](./02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md), together with a
  topic map so you can see exactly which skills to review.

## Problem 1: Adding and Subtracting Integers

Evaluate: $-9 + 14 - (-3)$

(A) 8  (B) -8  (C) 2  (D) -2  (E) 20

## Problem 2: Order of Operations With Whole Numbers

Evaluate: $6 + 4 \times (9 - 5) \div 2$

(A) 8  (B) 14  (C) 20  (D) 22  (E) 11

## Problem 3: Adding and Subtracting Decimals

Evaluate: $4.75 + 2.3 - 1.05$

(A) 8.1  (B) 5.95  (C) 6  (D) 6.1  (E) 7.1

## Problem 4: Multiplying Fractions

Evaluate: $\dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{3}{8}$

(A) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  (B) $\dfrac{5}{24}$  (C) $\dfrac{6}{11}$  (D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$  (E) $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Problem 5: Unit Rates

A car travels 240 miles on 8 gallons of gas. What is the unit rate in miles per gallon?

(A) 8 mpg  (B) 240 mpg  (C) 32 mpg  (D) 20 mpg  (E) 30 mpg

## Problem 6: Solving Proportions

Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{x}{20}$

(A) 12  (B) 15  (C) 9  (D) 60  (E) 4

## Problem 7: Proportional Relationships

$y$ is proportional to $x$. When $x = 4$, $y = 10$. Find $y$ when $x = 6$.

(A) 12  (B) 15  (C) 2.5  (D) 16  (E) 24

## Problem 8: Percent of a Number

What is 40% of 85?

(A) 45  (B) 21.25  (C) 34  (D) 40  (E) 3.4

## Problem 9: Percent Increase (Sales Tax)

A \$60 jacket has 8% sales tax added. What is the total price?

(A) \$60.08  (B) \$68  (C) \$55.20  (D) \$64.80  (E) \$4.80

## Problem 10: Percent Decrease (Discount)

A \$50 item is on sale for 20% off. What is the sale price?

(A) \$10  (B) \$30  (C) \$45  (D) \$20  (E) \$40

## Problem 11: Evaluating Expressions

Evaluate $2x + 5$ for $x = -3$.

(A) -1  (B) 11  (C) 1  (D) -11  (E) -6

## Problem 12: Combining Like Terms

Simplify: $7y - 3 + 2y + 8$

(A) $5y + 5$  (B) $9y + 5$  (C) $9y + 11$  (D) $5y + 11$  (E) $9y - 5$

## Problem 13: One-Step Equations

Solve for $x$: $-3x = 21$

(A) 7  (B) -18  (C) -7  (D) 24  (E) 18

## Problem 14: Circumference of a Circle

Find the circumference of a circle with radius 7 cm. (Use $\pi \approx \dfrac{22}{7}$)

(A) 22 cm  (B) 154 cm  (C) 14 cm  (D) 44 cm  (E) 49 cm

## Problem 15: Area of a Circle

Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm. (Use $\pi \approx \dfrac{22}{7}$)

(A) 44 cm²  (B) 49 cm²  (C) 22 cm²  (D) 98 cm²  (E) 154 cm²

## Problem 16: Area of a Trapezoid

Find the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 8 cm and 12 cm and height 5 cm.

(A) 50 cm²  (B) 100 cm²  (C) 20 cm²  (D) 25 cm²  (E) 40 cm²

## Problem 17: Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

Find the surface area of a rectangular prism with length 4 cm, width 3 cm, and height 2 cm.

(A) 24 cm²  (B) 52 cm²  (C) 26 cm²  (D) 20 cm²  (E) 46 cm²

## Problem 18: Mean

Find the mean of the data set: 4, 8, 6, 10, 2

(A) 8  (B) 5  (C) 6  (D) 10  (E) 30

## Problem 19: Simple Probability

A bag has 4 red, 3 blue, and 5 green marbles. What is the probability of drawing a blue
marble?

(A) $\dfrac{1}{3}$  (B) $\dfrac{3}{5}$  (C) $\dfrac{5}{12}$  (D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$  (E) $\dfrac{4}{12}$

## Problem 20: Coordinate Plane Quadrants

In which quadrant does the point $(5, -2)$ lie?

(A) Quadrant I  (B) Quadrant II  (C) Quadrant III  (D) It lies on an axis  (E) Quadrant IV
