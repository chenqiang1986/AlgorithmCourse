## Full Solutions

### Problem 1: Adding and Subtracting Integers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding its positive:

$$-9 + 14 - (-3) = -9 + 14 + 3 = 8$$

The answer is **(A) 8**.

</details>

### Problem 2: Order of Operations With Whole Numbers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Parentheses first, then multiplication/division left to right, then addition:

$$6 + 4 \times (9 - 5) \div 2 = 6 + 4 \times 4 \div 2 = 6 + 16 \div 2 = 6 + 8 = 14$$

The answer is **(B) 14**.

</details>

### Problem 3: Adding and Subtracting Decimals

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Line up the decimal points and work left to right:

$$4.75 + 2.3 - 1.05 = 7.05 - 1.05 = 6$$

The answer is **(C) 6**.

</details>

### Problem 4: Multiplying Fractions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply numerators and denominators, then simplify:

$$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{8} = \frac{6}{24} = \frac{1}{4}$$

The answer is **(D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$**.

</details>

### Problem 5: Unit Rates

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide the total distance by the number of gallons:

$$\frac{240 \text{ miles}}{8 \text{ gallons}} = 30 \text{ mpg}$$

The answer is **(E) 30 mpg**.

</details>

### Problem 6: Solving Proportions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Cross-multiply, then solve for $x$:

$$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{x}{20} \implies 5x = 60 \implies x = 12$$

The answer is **(A) 12**.

</details>

### Problem 7: Proportional Relationships

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Find the constant of proportionality $k = y \div x$, then use it to find the missing value:

$$k = \frac{10}{4} = 2.5 \implies y = 2.5 \times 6 = 15$$

The answer is **(B) 15**.

</details>

### Problem 8: Percent of a Number

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Convert 40% to the decimal 0.4 and multiply:

$$0.4 \times 85 = 34$$

The answer is **(C) 34**.

</details>

### Problem 9: Percent Increase (Sales Tax)

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Find the tax amount, then add it to the original price:

$$60 \times 0.08 = 4.80 \implies 60 + 4.80 = 64.80$$

The answer is **(D) \$64.80**.

</details>

### Problem 10: Percent Decrease (Discount)

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price:

$$50 \times 0.20 = 10 \implies 50 - 10 = 40$$

The answer is **(E) \$40**.

</details>

### Problem 11: Evaluating Expressions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Substitute $x = -3$:

$$2x + 5 = 2(-3) + 5 = -6 + 5 = -1$$

The answer is **(A) -1**.

</details>

### Problem 12: Combining Like Terms

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Group the $y$ terms and the constants separately:

$$7y - 3 + 2y + 8 = (7y + 2y) + (-3 + 8) = 9y + 5$$

The answer is **(B) $9y+5$**.

</details>

### Problem 13: One-Step Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide both sides by $-3$:

$$-3x = 21 \implies x = \frac{21}{-3} = -7$$

The answer is **(C) -7**.

</details>

### Problem 14: Circumference of a Circle

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Circumference is $2\pi r$:

$$C = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 = 44 \text{ cm}$$

The answer is **(D) 44 cm**.

</details>

### Problem 15: Area of a Circle

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Area is $\pi r^2$:

$$A = \frac{22}{7} \times 7^2 = \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 154 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(E) 154 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 16: Area of a Trapezoid

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Area is half the sum of the parallel sides times the height:

$$A = \frac{1}{2}(8 + 12) \times 5 = \frac{1}{2} \times 20 \times 5 = 50 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(A) 50 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 17: Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Add the areas of all three pairs of faces, then double:

$$SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2(4 \times 3 + 4 \times 2 + 3 \times 2) = 2(12 + 8 + 6) = 2 \times 26 = 52 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(B) 52 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 18: Mean

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Add the values and divide by how many there are:

$$\frac{4 + 8 + 6 + 10 + 2}{5} = \frac{30}{5} = 6$$

The answer is **(C) 6**.

</details>

### Problem 19: Simple Probability

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Probability is the number of favorable outcomes over the total number of outcomes. There are
$4 + 3 + 5 = 12$ marbles total, and 3 are blue:

$$\frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$$

The answer is **(D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$**.

</details>

### Problem 20: Coordinate Plane Quadrants

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Quadrant I has $(+,+)$, Quadrant II has $(-,+)$, Quadrant III has $(-,-)$, and Quadrant IV
has $(+,-)$. The point $(5, -2)$ has a positive $x$-coordinate and a negative
$y$-coordinate:

$$(5, -2) \implies (+, -) \implies \text{Quadrant IV}$$

The answer is **(E) Quadrant IV**.

</details>
