# Course 2

This folder contains Course 2 of the Low Level Math sequence — the standard-paced
7th-grade bridge between [Middle School Placement](../Middle%20School%20Placement) and
[Course 3](../Course%203), for students on the standard middle school track rather than
the accelerated pathway.

## Course Goals

Before any lessons are written, this module opens with a diagnostic test that samples the
full range of skills a student needs before starting Course 3:

1. Operations with rational numbers: integers, decimals, and fractions.
2. Ratios, rates, unit rates, and proportional relationships.
3. Percents: percent of a number, tax, and discount.
4. Evaluating expressions, combining like terms, and one-step equations.
5. Geometry: circumference and area of circles, area of a trapezoid, and surface area of
   a rectangular prism.
6. Probability, statistics (mean), and the coordinate plane.
