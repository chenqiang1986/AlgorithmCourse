# Course 2

This folder contains Course 2 of the Low Level Math sequence — the standard-paced
7th-grade bridge between [Middle School Placement](../Middle%20School%20Placement) and
[Course 3](../Course%203), for students on the standard middle school track rather than
the accelerated pathway.

## Course Goals

The full 36-week scope and sequence lives in
[00-syllabus.md](./00-syllabus.md). Before any lessons are written, this module opens with
a diagnostic test that samples the full range of skills a student needs before starting
Course 3:

1. Negative integers and the sign rules for adding, subtracting, multiplying, and
   dividing them.
2. Operations with rational numbers: integers, decimals, and fractions.
3. Ratios, rates, unit rates, and proportional relationships.
4. Percents: percent of a number, tax, and discount.
5. Evaluating expressions, combining like terms, and one-step equations.
6. Geometry: circumference and area of circles, area of a trapezoid, and surface area of
   a rectangular prism.
7. Probability, statistics (mean), and the coordinate plane.

## Lessons

1. [00-syllabus.md](./00-syllabus.md)
   The 36-week syllabus: six units spanning rational number operations, ratios and
   proportional relationships, percentages, expressions and equations, geometry and scale
   drawings, and statistics and probability.
2. [01-diagnostic-test.md](./01-diagnostic-test.md)
   A 20-question, ~40-minute diagnostic test covering all major Course 2 topics. Answer
   key and full solutions: [02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md](./02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md).
3. [03-negative-integers-addition-subtraction.md](./03-negative-integers-addition-subtraction.md)
   Introduces negative integers and absolute value, then the rules for adding and
   subtracting integers, worked through both a number line model and the sign-rule
   template.
4. [04-integer-multiplication-division.md](./04-integer-multiplication-division.md)
   Sign rules for multiplying and dividing integers, including why a negative times a
   negative is positive, and handling three or more factors.
5. [04-homework-integer-operations.md](./04-homework-integer-operations.md)
   A 20-problem homework set covering Lessons 3–4: adding, subtracting, multiplying, and
   dividing integers, plus combined expressions using all four operations together.
6. [05-fraction-multiplication-division.md](./05-fraction-multiplication-division.md)
   Introduces the fraction as the exact value of a division that doesn't come out even,
   then covers fraction sign rules, multiplication, and division (including the
   reciprocal).
7. [06-fraction-addition.md](./06-fraction-addition.md)
   Adding fractions with the same denominator using the pizza model, then introduces
   equivalent fractions via the product rule ($a/b = ac/bc$) to add fractions with
   different denominators, and reducing the result to simplest form by running that same
   rule in reverse.
8. [06-homework-fraction-operations.md](./06-homework-fraction-operations.md)
   A 20-problem homework set covering Lessons 5–6: fraction signs, multiplication, and
   division; adding fractions with same and different denominators and reducing to
   simplest form; plus composite expressions that combine multiplication and addition.

More lessons can be added later as the course grows.
