# Algebra I Diagnostic Test

This test samples every major Algebra I topic to identify which skills a student already
has and which need review before starting the course.

## How to Use This Test

- Time limit: **40 minutes**. No calculator.
- Attempt every problem — if you're unsure, mark your best guess and move on rather than
  spending more than 2 minutes on any one question.
- This is a placement tool, not a graded exam. The answer key and full solutions live in
  [02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md](./02-diagnostic-test-solutions.md), together with a
  topic map so you can see exactly which skills to review.

## Problem 1: Order of Operations

Evaluate: $7 + 3(5 - 2)^2 \div 3$

(A) 9  (B) 16  (C) 25  (D) 30  (E) 48

## Problem 2: Combining Like Terms

Simplify: $4x + 7 - 2x + 3$

(A) $2x + 10$  (B) $6x + 10$  (C) $2x + 4$  (D) $6x + 4$  (E) $2x - 4$

## Problem 3: One-Step / Two-Step Equations

Solve for $x$: $3x - 5 = 16$

(A) 5  (B) 7  (C) 9  (D) 11  (E) 21

## Problem 4: Variables on Both Sides

Solve for $x$: $2(x + 3) = 5x - 6$

(A) -4  (B) 0  (C) 4  (D) 6  (E) 12

## Problem 5: Translating Word Problems

Five less than twice a number is 13. Find the number.

(A) 4  (B) 6  (C) 9  (D) 11  (E) 14

## Problem 6: Solving Inequalities

Solve for $x$: $3x + 4 < 19$

(A) $x < 5$  (B) $x > 5$  (C) $x < 15$  (D) $x > -5$  (E) $x < 1$

## Problem 7: Exponent Rules — Product

Simplify: $x^3 \cdot x^5$

(A) $x^8$  (B) $x^{15}$  (C) $x^2$  (D) $2x^8$  (E) $x^{-2}$

## Problem 8: Exponent Rules — Power of a Power

Simplify: $(2x^2)^3$

(A) $2x^6$  (B) $6x^6$  (C) $8x^5$  (D) $8x^6$  (E) $6x^5$

## Problem 9: Exponent Rules — Quotient and Negative Exponents

Simplify: $\dfrac{x^4}{x^7}$

(A) $x^3$  (B) $\dfrac{1}{x^3}$  (C) $-x^3$  (D) $x^{11}$  (E) $\dfrac{1}{x^{11}}$

## Problem 10: Adding Polynomials

Simplify: $(3x^2 - 2x + 5) + (x^2 + 4x - 7)$

(A) $4x^2 + 2x - 2$  (B) $4x^2 - 2x - 2$  (C) $2x^2 + 2x - 2$  (D) $4x^2 + 2x + 2$  (E) $3x^2 + 2x - 2$

## Problem 11: Multiplying Binomials

Multiply: $(x + 3)(x - 5)$

(A) $x^2 - 2x - 15$  (B) $x^2 + 2x - 15$  (C) $x^2 - 2x + 15$  (D) $x^2 - 8x - 15$  (E) $x^2 - 2x - 8$

## Problem 12: Factoring a Trinomial

Factor: $x^2 + 7x + 12$

(A) $(x+3)(x+4)$  (B) $(x+2)(x+6)$  (C) $(x+1)(x+12)$  (D) $(x-3)(x-4)$  (E) $(x+3)(x-4)$

## Problem 13: Factoring a Difference of Squares

Factor: $x^2 - 49$

(A) $(x-7)^2$  (B) $(x-7)(x+7)$  (C) $(x+7)^2$  (D) $(x-49)(x+1)$  (E) cannot be factored

## Problem 14: Simplifying Radicals

Simplify: $\sqrt{72}$

(A) $6\sqrt{2}$  (B) $8\sqrt{3}$  (C) $2\sqrt{18}$  (D) $36\sqrt{2}$  (E) $6\sqrt{12}$

## Problem 15: Slope From Two Points

Find the slope of the line through $(2, 3)$ and $(6, 11)$.

(A) 2  (B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  (C) 4  (D) -2  (E) 8

## Problem 16: Equation of a Line

Find the equation of the line with slope $-2$ that passes through $(1, 5)$.

(A) $y = -2x + 7$  (B) $y = -2x + 3$  (C) $y = 2x + 7$  (D) $y = -2x - 7$  (E) $y = -2x + 5$

## Problem 17: Evaluating a Function

If $f(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$, find $f(-2)$.

(A) 15  (B) 3  (C) -15  (D) 9  (E) 11

## Problem 18: Solving a System of Equations

Solve the system:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + y &= 10 \\
x - y &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

(A) $(6, 4)$  (B) $(4, 6)$  (C) $(5, 5)$  (D) $(8, 2)$  (E) $(6, -4)$

## Problem 19: Direct Proportion Word Problem

A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours. At the same rate, how many miles will it travel in 7 hours?

(A) 300  (B) 350  (C) 400  (D) 450  (E) 500

## Problem 20: Applied System of Equations

A theater sells adult tickets for \$8 and child tickets for \$5. It sells 100 tickets total
and collects \$650. How many adult tickets were sold?

(A) 40  (B) 50  (C) 60  (D) 70  (E) 80
