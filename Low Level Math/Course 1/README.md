# Course 1
*Low Level Math / Course 1*

This folder contains Course 1 of the Low Level Math sequence — the standard-paced
6th-grade course that precedes [Course 2](../Course%202).

## Course Goals

The full 36-week scope and sequence lives in
[00-syllabus.md](./00-syllabus.md). Before any lessons are written, this module opens with
a diagnostic test that samples the full range of skills a student needs before starting
Course 1:

1. Ratios and proportional relationships: ratio language, unit rates, and real-world
   percentage and scaling problems.
2. The number system: fraction and decimal fluency, rational numbers, and the coordinate
   plane.
3. Expressions, equations, and inequalities: writing and evaluating expressions, and
   solving one-step equations and inequalities.
4. Geometry: area of polygons, volume of right rectangular prisms, and surface area from
   3D nets.
5. Statistics and probability: measures of center and variability, and displaying data
   with dot plots, histograms, and box plots.
