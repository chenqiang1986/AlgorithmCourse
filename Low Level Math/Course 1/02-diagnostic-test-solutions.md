## Full Solutions

### Problem 1: Ratio Language

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

A ratio of boys to girls lists boys first: $12 : 18$. Divide both terms by their greatest
common factor, 6:

$$12 : 18 = 2 : 3$$

The answer is **(A) 2:3**.

</details>

### Problem 2: Unit Rates

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide the amount of flour by the number of cookies:

$$\frac{5 \text{ cups}}{20 \text{ cookies}} = 0.25 \text{ cups/cookie}$$

The answer is **(B) 0.25 cups/cookie**.

</details>

### Problem 3: Ratio Tables and Equivalent Ratios

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

12 pencils is 4 times as many as 3 pencils, so multiply the cost by 4 as well:

$$3 \text{ pencils} : \$2 \implies 12 \text{ pencils} : 4 \times \$2 = \$8$$

The answer is **(C) \$8**.

</details>

### Problem 4: Scaling Real-World Quantities

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Find the amount of rice per person, then scale up to 10 people:

$$\frac{6 \text{ cups}}{4 \text{ people}} = 1.5 \text{ cups/person} \implies 1.5 \times 10 = 15 \text{ cups}$$

The answer is **(D) 15**.

</details>

### Problem 5: Multi-Digit Division

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Divide step by step, or check by multiplying back:

$$4536 \div 24 = 189 \quad (\text{check: } 24 \times 189 = 4536)$$

The answer is **(E) 189**.

</details>

### Problem 6: Dividing Fractions by Fractions

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{15}{8}$$

The answer is **(A) $\dfrac{15}{8}$**.

</details>

### Problem 7: Ordering Rational Numbers

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

On a number line, more negative numbers come first, then zero, then positive numbers in
increasing order:

$$-5 < -3 < 0 < 2 < 4$$

The answer is **(B) $-5, -3, 0, 2, 4$**.

</details>

### Problem 8: Coordinate Plane Quadrants

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Quadrant I has $(+,+)$, Quadrant II has $(-,+)$, Quadrant III has $(-,-)$, and Quadrant IV
has $(+,-)$. The point $(-4, 3)$ has a negative $x$-coordinate and a positive
$y$-coordinate:

$$(-4, 3) \implies (-, +) \implies \text{Quadrant II}$$

The answer is **(C) Quadrant II**.

</details>

### Problem 9: Order of Operations With Exponents

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Evaluate the exponent and the parentheses first, then multiply, then add:

$$3^2 + 4 \times (7 - 3) = 9 + 4 \times 4 = 9 + 16 = 25$$

The answer is **(D) 25**.

</details>

### Problem 10: The Distributive Property

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply 4 by each term inside the parentheses:

$$4(x + 5) = 4x + 20$$

The answer is **(E) $4x + 20$**.

</details>

### Problem 11: One-Step Equations

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply both sides by 6:

$$\frac{x}{6} = 9 \implies x = 9 \times 6 = 54$$

The answer is **(A) 54**.

</details>

### Problem 12: One-Step Inequalities

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Subtract 7 from both sides:

$$x + 7 \le 15 \implies x \le 8$$

The answer is **(B) $x \le 8$**.

</details>

### Problem 13: Area of a Triangle

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Area of a triangle is half the base times the height:

$$A = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 6 = 30 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(C) 30 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 14: Area of a Parallelogram

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Area of a parallelogram is base times height:

$$A = 9 \times 4 = 36 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(D) 36 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 15: Volume of a Rectangular Prism

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Volume is length times width times height:

$$V = 2\tfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 3 = 2.5 \times 2 \times 3 = 15 \text{ in}^3$$

The answer is **(E) 15 in³**.

</details>

### Problem 16: Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Add the areas of all three pairs of faces, then double:

$$SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2(5 \times 3 + 5 \times 2 + 3 \times 2) = 2(15 + 10 + 6) = 2 \times 31 = 62 \text{ cm}^2$$

The answer is **(A) 62 cm²**.

</details>

### Problem 17: Median

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Sort the data, then take the middle value:

$$2, 4, 5, 7, 9 \implies \text{median} = 5$$

The answer is **(B) 5**.

</details>

### Problem 18: Interquartile Range

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Split the sorted data into a lower half and an upper half around the median (12), find each
half's median, then subtract:

$$3, 7, 8 \mid 12 \mid 14, 18, 21 \implies Q_1 = 7, \ Q_3 = 18 \implies IQR = 18 - 7 = 11$$

The answer is **(C) 11**.

</details>

### Problem 19: Mode From a Dot Plot

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

The mode is the value that appears most often. In $0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4$, the value
1 appears three times, more than any other value:

$$\text{mode} = 1$$

The answer is **(D) 1**.

</details>

### Problem 20: Range From a Box Plot

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Range is the maximum minus the minimum:

$$\text{range} = 35 - 10 = 25$$

The answer is **(E) 25**.

</details>
