# Algebra II Syllabus

A year-long Algebra II syllabus covering the eight units below, four per semester.

## Semester 1

### Unit 1: Linear Systems & Function Foundations

- Review of linear equations, inequalities, and absolute value functions.
- Systems of linear equations in 2 and 3 variables (substitution, elimination, matrices).
- Function notation, domain and range, interval notation.
- Transformations of functions ($f(x) \to a f(x - h) + k$).
- Composite and inverse functions ($f(g(x))$ and $f^{-1}(x)$).

### Unit 2: Complex Numbers & Quadratic Functions

- Operations with complex numbers ($i = \sqrt{-1}$, $a + bi$).
- Solving quadratics over the complex number system (factoring, completing the square,
  Quadratic Formula).
- Vertex, standard, and intercept forms of quadratics.
- Systems of nonlinear equations (linear-quadratic systems).

### Unit 3: Polynomial Functions

- Polynomial arithmetic (addition, subtraction, multiplication, long division, synthetic
  division).
- Factoring polynomials (grouping, sum/difference of cubes, higher-degree trinomials).
- Finding roots/zeros: Remainder Theorem, Factor Theorem, Rational Root Theorem, and
  Fundamental Theorem of Algebra.
- Analyzing polynomial graphs: end behavior, turning points, local extrema, and
  multiplicity.

### Unit 4: Radical Functions & Rational Exponents

- Rational exponents and radical equivalences ($x^{a/b} = \sqrt[b]{x^a}$).
- Simplifying, multiplying, and dividing radical expressions.
- Graphing square root and cube root functions.
- Solving radical equations and identifying extraneous solutions.

## Semester 2

### Unit 5: Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

- Exponential growth and decay models ($y = a \cdot b^x$, $A = P e^{rt}$).
- Introduction to logarithms as inverses of exponentials
  ($\log_b(x) = y \iff b^y = x$).
- Properties of logarithms (product, quotient, power rules, change of base).
- Natural base ($e$) and natural logarithm ($\ln$).
- Solving exponential and logarithmic equations and applications (compound interest,
  population models).

### Unit 6: Rational Functions

- Simplifying, multiplying, dividing, adding, and subtracting rational expressions.
- Graphing rational functions: identifying vertical/horizontal asymptotes, holes, domain
  restrictions, and end behavior.
- Solving rational equations and checking for extraneous roots.

### Unit 7: Trigonometry & Periodic Functions

- Right triangle trigonometry review (sine, cosine, tangent).
- Angles in standard position, radian measure, and degree-to-radian conversions.
- The Unit Circle and evaluation of trigonometric functions at special angles.
- Graphing sine and cosine functions (amplitude, period, phase shift, vertical shift).
- Fundamental identities (Pythagorean identity $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$).

### Unit 8: Sequences, Series, & Statistics / Probability

- **Sequences & Series:** Arithmetic and geometric sequences (explicit and recursive
  rules), finite and infinite geometric series.
- **Combinatorics & Probability:** Fundamental Counting Principle, permutations,
  combinations, and binomial expansion (Binomial Theorem).
- **Statistics:** Normal distribution, $z$-scores, standard deviation, and observational
  studies vs. experiments.
